Section A · what to look at again

Question 1 · MCQ

Classifying resources as individual, community, national and international is a classification on the basis of:

Correct: (c)

Ownership gives individual, community, national and international. Origin gives biotic and abiotic; exhaustibility gives renewable and non-renewable; status of development gives potential, developed stock and reserves.

Question 2 · MCQ

About 27 per cent of India's area is plateau. According to your textbook, the plateau region:

Correct: (a)

The plateau possesses rich reserves of minerals, fossil fuels and forests. Perennial rivers come from the mountains; agriculture and industry are the plains' facilities; Himalayan river deposits form the alluvial plains.

Question 3 · MCQ

Its name comes from a Latin word meaning brick. It is acidic, with a pH below 6.0. It is humus rich where it supports deciduous and evergreen forests. Which soil is it?

Correct: (b)

"Later" means brick; laterite is acidic (pH below 6.0) and humus rich under deciduous and evergreen forests. Black soil is the clayey cotton soil of the Deccan trap; arid soil lacks humus; forest soil is acidic only in snow-covered areas and its name is not from "later".

Question 4 · MCQ

Which of the following is included in "land put to non-agricultural uses"?

Correct: (d)

Settlements, roads, railways and industry are land put to non-agricultural uses. Rocky, arid and desert areas are waste land; pastures and tree crop groves are "other uncultivated land".

Question 5 · A–R
AArid soils become cultivable after proper irrigation, as has happened in western Rajasthan.
RIn some areas of arid soil, common salt is obtained by evaporating the water.

Correct: (B)

Both are true (arid soils paragraph), but salt being obtained from the soil does not explain why irrigation makes it cultivable.

Question 6 · A–R
AStrip cropping is a useful method of soil conservation on open, windy farmland.
RGrass strips grown between the crops break up the force of the wind.

Correct: (A)

Both are true, and R explains A: strips of grass break the force of the wind, which is why the method suits windy land.
