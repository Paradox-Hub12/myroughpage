Section A · what to look at again

Question 1 · MCQ

Which of these is one of the photographs in NCERT Fig. 2.1, which shows people and forests?

Correct: (b)

Fig. 2.1 has three photographs: tribal girls using bamboo saplings in a nursery at Mukhali near Silent Valley, tribal women selling minor forest produce, and leaf litter collection by women folk. Rhino and deer in Kaziranga is Fig. 2.2; the gharial is in a newspaper clipping on p. 16; no figure shows a JFM meeting.

Question 2 · MCQ

Which of these does your textbook list among the animals given full or partial legal protection most recently?

Correct: (a)

Snow leopard is in the "most recently" list with the Indian elephant, black buck and great Indian bustard; the other three had special projects.

Question 3 · MCQ

Your textbook's chapter opens with a folk song that offers the music of springs, rivers, mountains, forests, insects and animals to Narak. Whose folk song is it?

Correct: (c)

The chapter opens with a Lepcha folk song to Narak, "the creator of music". Tehri, Alwar and the Chota Nagpur region appear later in the chapter, with the Beej Bachao Andolan, the Bhairodev Dakav 'Sonchuri' and sacred trees.

Question 4 · MCQ

When, according to your textbook, did conservationists demand a national wildlife protection programme?

Correct: (d)

Conservationists demanded a national wildlife protection programme in the 1960s and 1970s; the Act followed in 1972. Project Tiger came in 1973, Joint Forest Management in 1988, and the first plants on the protected list in 1991.

Question 5 · A–R
ABihar, Haryana and Punjab are among the states with most of their forest classed as protected forest.
RProtected forests make up more than half of India's forest land.

Correct: (C)

A is true: Bihar, Haryana and Punjab are in the textbook's list of states with a bulk of their forest under protected forests. R is false: protected forests are almost one-third of the forest area; more than half is reserved forest.

Question 6 · A–R
AEven insects now find a place in conservation planning in India.
RConservation projects now focus on biodiversity rather than on a few of its components.

Correct: (A)

Both are true, and R explains A: because projects now cover the whole of biodiversity, insects (and later plants) were added to the protected list.
