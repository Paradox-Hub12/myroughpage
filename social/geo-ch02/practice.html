An imaginary district, Hariyali, has 1,250 sq km of forest: 700 sq km reserved, 400 sq km protected and 150 sq km unclassed. (a) Find the share of each kind in per cent. (b) How much of Hariyali's forest is permanent forest estate? (c) Does Hariyali follow the pattern your textbook gives for India?

(Hariyali and its numbers are imagined for practice; the India pattern is from your textbook.)

Full solution

Asked: three shares, one total and a comparison.

Concept used: Three kinds of forest; permanent forest estates (Topic 4).

  1. 1

    (a) Reserved 700 ÷ 1,250 × 100 = 56 per cent; protected 400 ÷ 1,250 × 100 = 32 per cent; unclassed 150 ÷ 1,250 × 100 = 12 per cent.

  2. 2

    (b) Permanent forest estate = reserved + protected = 700 + 400 = 1,100 sq km (88 per cent).

  3. 3

    (c) Yes: reserved is more than half, protected is about one-third, unclassed is the smallest.

  4. Check: 56 + 32 + 12 = 100 ✔; 700 + 400 + 150 = 1,250 ✔.

  5. Answer (a) 56, 32 and 12 per cent; (b) 1,100 sq km; (c) yes.

    Write it in the exam (3 marks):

    1. (a) Reserved 56 per cent, protected 32 per cent and unclassed 12 per cent. (1)

    3. (c) Yes: the pattern matches India's — more than half, about a third, and the smallest share last. (1)
