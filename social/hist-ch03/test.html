Section A · what to look at again

Question 1 · MCQ

Box 1 in your textbook quotes a letter of May 1634 by the first governor of a colony in New England. Which colony?

Correct: (b)

Box 1 quotes John Winthorp, the first governor of the Massachusetts Bay colony in New England, writing in May 1634. New Orleans is the place of NCERT Fig. 5; Peru is where silver mines were; Virginia is not in your textbook.

Question 2 · MCQ

Arrange in chronological order, earliest first:
I. Ships start to be carved often on memorial stones along India's western coast.
II. Germs that carry disease are first known to travel between distant lands.
III. Legends of a fabled city of gold send expeditions out of Europe.
IV. A famine strikes Ireland when its poorest families' main food crop fails.

Correct: (c)

II seventh century → I from the ninth century → III seventeenth century → IV mid-1840s. Option (a) puts the ships before the germs; (b) puts the legends before the ships; (d) puts the famine before the legends.

Question 3 · MCQ

Which of these statements about the sources in this chapter are correct?
I. NCERT Fig. 4 shows hungry children digging in a field that has already been harvested.
II. NCERT Fig. 5 was published in the Illustrated London News in 1851.
III. NCERT Fig. 1 is a Chinese cave painting.
IV. Box 1 is taken from Alfred Crosby's Ecological Imperialism.

Correct: (d)

I, II and IV match the captions and Box 1. III is false: Fig. 1 is a ship on a tenth-century memorial stone in the Goa Museum; the Chinese cave painting is Fig. 2.

Question 4 · A–R
AArab traders certainly took pasta to fifth-century Sicily.
RSimilar foods were also known in India and Japan.

Correct: (D)

A is false: your textbook says "perhaps Arab traders took pasta to fifth-century Sicily" — it is not certain. R is true, and it is one reason the truth about these foods' origins may never be known.

Question 5 · A–R
AReligious dissenters were among the thousands who fled Europe for America.
RA dissenter is one who accepts established beliefs and practices.

Correct: (C)

A is true: dissenters were persecuted, and thousands therefore fled Europe for America. R is false: a dissenter is one who refuses to accept established beliefs and practices.

Question 6 · MCQ

NCERT Fig. 2 shows silk route trade in an eighth-century painting. Where is this painting?

Correct: (a)

Fig. 2's caption: "Cave 217, Mogao Grottoes, Gansu, China". The Goa Museum holds the stone of Fig. 1; Venice appears in Fig. 3; New Orleans in Fig. 5.

Question 7 · MCQ

Which of these did not travel the silk routes, according to your textbook?

Correct: (d)

Potatoes came from America and reached Europe and Asia only after Columbus — long after the silk routes' great age. Silk, Chinese pottery and spices from Southeast Asia all travelled the silk routes.
