P13●● StandardShort answer · 3 marksOriginal

Asked to "Suggest three measures to conserve water in a farming district", a student wrote:

  1. Irrigated agriculture is the largest consumer of water.
  2. Tube-wells may lower the groundwater level.
  3. Farmers have shifted to water intensive crops.

What is wrong with the answer? Rewrite it with three measures from your textbook.

Full solution

Asked: the fault, and three real measures.

Concept used: Cause → measure (Topics 2, 4 and 5).

  1. 1

    The fault: all three lines are causes of the problem; the question asks for measures (cures).

  2. 2

    Measure 1: develop drought resistant crops and dry farming techniques.

  3. 3

    Measure 2: use water saving irrigation technologies — "per drop more crop" — and improve on-farm water use efficiency (PMKSY).

  4. 4

    Measure 3: harvest rainwater in the fields, as khadins and johads do in Rajasthan, so water stands and moistens the soil.

  5. Check: each new point starts with an action verb — develop, use, harvest — which is a quick sign of a measure.

  6. Answer The student wrote causes; measures are drought resistant crops and dry farming, water saving irrigation, and rain fed storage in fields.

    Write it in the exam (3 marks):

    1. Develop drought resistant crops and dry farming techniques. (1)
    2. Use water saving irrigation technologies ("per drop more crop") to improve on-farm water use efficiency. (1)
    3. Harvest rainwater in fields, as khadins and johads do, so water stands and moistens the soil. (1)
    Common trap: turning a cause into a "measure" by adding "should not" ("tube-wells should not lower water"). Name an action that saves water.

P19●● StandardShort answer · 3 marksOriginal

Full solution

Asked: three harms to the river and its life.

Concept used: Why big dams came under opposition (Topic 4).

    Flow and silt: damming affects the natural flow, causing poor sediment flow downstream and excessive sedimentation at the bottom of the reservoir.

  2. 2

    Stream beds and habitats: this results in rockier stream beds and poorer habitats for the river's aquatic life.

  3. 3

    Fragmentation: dams fragment rivers, making it difficult for aquatic fauna to migrate, especially for spawning.

  4. Check: flow, habitat and migration — three different harms, all from the textbook.

  5. Answer Natural and sediment flow disturbed; rockier stream beds and poorer habitats; fish cannot migrate to spawn.

    Write it in the exam (3 marks):

    1. Damming affects natural flow, causing poor sediment flow and excessive sedimentation at the bottom of the reservoir. (1)
    2. This leaves rockier stream beds and poorer habitats for aquatic life. (1)
    3. Dams fragment rivers, so aquatic fauna find it difficult to migrate, especially for spawning. (1)
    Common trap: writing "dams make the water salty". Salinisation affects soil after a shift to water intensive crops.
