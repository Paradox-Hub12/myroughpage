Section A · what to look at again

Question 1 · MCQ

In NCERT Fig. 3.1 ("Water Scarcity"), a boy collects drinking water after a heavy downpour that flooded a city. Which city is it?

Correct: (b)

The Fig. 3.1 caption reads that after a heavy downpour a boy collects drinking water in Kolkata, where a record overnight rain of 180 mm flooded a vast area. Shillong, Jaisalmer and Mysuru appear elsewhere in the chapter, not in Fig. 3.1.

Question 2 · MCQ

NCERT Fig. 3.4 is a poster that asks, "Are you a water harvester?" What does it invite people to do this monsoon?

Correct: (a)

The poster reads: "Are you a water harvester? This monsoon, join us in counting the raindrops." It invites people to harvest rain, not to build dams, dig tube-wells or pay a tax.

Question 3 · MCQ

A Bhadu song printed in your textbook begs a river to "reduce the floods a little". Which river does the textbook call "the river of sorrow"?

Correct: (c)

The note under the Bhadu song says it describes the troubles caused by the flooding of the Damodar river, "known as the river of sorrow". The Mahanadi, Krishna and Chambal are dam rivers in this chapter, but none is called the river of sorrow.

Question 4 · MCQ

Your textbook's box on hydraulic structures in ancient India names Nagarjunakonda for its irrigation works. In which state does the box place it?

Correct: (d)

The box lists irrigation works in Kalinga (Odisha), Nagarjunakonda (Andhra Pradesh), Bennur (Karnataka) and Kolhapur (Maharashtra).

Question 5 · A–R
AReservoirs created on floodplains lead to the decomposition of vegetation and soil over a period of time.
RThese reservoirs submerge the existing vegetation and soil.

Correct: (A)

Both are true, and R explains A: the reservoirs submerge the vegetation and soil, which is why they decompose over time.

Question 6 · A–R
AIn the tankas of Rajasthan, the first spell of rain was usually not collected.
RTankas were filled only with water from canals.

Correct: (C)

A is true: the first spell of rain cleaned the roofs and pipes, so it was not collected. R is false: tankas were filled with rainwater from the sloping roofs through a pipe; the Indira Gandhi Canal is the reason the practice is now declining.
