Week 1

Set 2 — Week 1

5 questions, about 8 minutes

Focus: the reasons behind the facts.

W1.1 · 1 of 5●● Standard

Why can a city with plenty of water still face water scarcity?

Answer: Because water is over-exploited and unequally shared among a large, growing population, and much of it may be polluted and hazardous for human use. (Topic 1)

Did you get it?

W1.2 · 2 of 5●● Standard

Why does the growth of industries put double pressure on water?

Answer: Industries are heavy users of water, and they need power, much of it hydroelectric, which also depends on water. (Topic 2)

Did you get it?

W1.3 · 3 of 5●● Standard

Why did Jawaharlal Nehru call dams the "temples of modern India"?

Answer: Because they would integrate the development of agriculture and the village economy with rapid industrialisation and the growth of the urban economy. (Topic 3)

Did you get it?

W1.4 · 4 of 5●● Standard

Why is rooftop rainwater harvesting declining in western Rajasthan?

Answer: Because plenty of water is now available from the perennial Indira Gandhi Canal (some houses still keep tankas because they dislike the taste of tap water). (Topic 5)

Did you get it?

W1.5 · 5 of 5●● Standard

How does a dam make life difficult for fish and other aquatic fauna?

Answer: Dams fragment rivers, so aquatic fauna find it difficult to migrate, especially for spawning; poor sediment flow also leaves poorer habitats. (Topic 4)

Did you get it?

Month 1

Set 3 — Month 1

5 questions, about 12 minutes

Focus: using what you know in new ways.

M1.1 · 1 of 5●● Standard

Arrange from north to south: Rana Pratap Sagar, Salal, Nagarjuna Sagar, Bhakra Nangal.

Answer: Salal → Bhakra Nangal → Rana Pratap Sagar → Nagarjuna Sagar. (Topic 7)

Did you get it?

M1.2 · 2 of 5●● Standard

A friend says, "The Jal Jeevan Mission and PMKSY do the same job." Give one difference between them.

Answer: JJM brings potable piped drinking water to rural households; PMKSY brings irrigation water to farms ("har khet ko pani", "per drop more crop"). (Topics 2 and 4)

Did you get it?

M1.3 · 3 of 5●● Standard

A canal turns a dry district green, but years later the soil shows a white crust. Use your textbook to explain the link.

Answer: Irrigation changed the cropping pattern: farmers shifted to water intensive and commercial crops, which has caused salinisation of the soil. (Topic 4)

Did you get it?

M1.4 · 4 of 5●● Standard

Give one advantage and one disadvantage of multi-purpose projects, from your textbook.

Answer: One possible pair — advantage: they give irrigation and hydel power; disadvantage: they have triggered floods through sedimentation (or fragmented rivers, caused salinisation, displaced people). (Topic 4)

Did you get it?

M1.5 · 5 of 5●● Standard

Name one traditional and one modern way of harvesting rooftop rain, each with its place.

Answer: Traditional: tankas in Bikaner, Phalodi and Barmer (Rajasthan). Modern: rooftop harvesting in Gendathur (Karnataka) or Shillong (Meghalaya), or compulsory in Tamil Nadu. (Topics 5 and 6)

Did you get it?
