Topic 7 of 7

Map work: the eight dams

About 30 minutes · 2 things to learn
By the end you can…
  • Locate and label the eight syllabus dams on an outline map of India, from the name or from a description.
  • Name the river of each dam (and, where asked, its state).

"The dam built on Mahanadi river." A short line in a board paper, a blank outline map, and one mark for a dot in the right place with the right name beside it. Every board paper of 2026 had a question like this. If you know each dam's river and where that river flows, the dot almost places itself.

Quick reminder (rivers and states)

From Class 9: the Chenab and the Satluj are rivers of the Indus system in the north-west; the Ganga rises in the Himalayas of Uttarakhand; the Chambal flows northwards through Rajasthan towards the Yamuna; the Narmada flows west through a rift valley into the Arabian Sea in Gujarat; the Mahanadi flows east through Odisha; the Krishna and its tributary the Tungabhadra flow east across the southern plateau. On an outline map, Jammu and Kashmir is at the top, Himachal Pradesh and Punjab below it, Uttarakhand to their east.

  1. How this map item is asked

  • The syllabus says "locate and label" these eight dams: Salal, Bhakra Nangal, Tehri, Rana Pratap Sagar, Sardar Sarovar, Hirakud, Nagarjuna Sagar, Tungabhadra.
  • Locate and label means: mark the place with a symbol on the outline map and write the dam's name beside it.
  • Board papers give either the name ("Hirakud – Dam") or a description ("The dam built on Mahanadi river", "The major dam constructed on the Chenab river", "The largest dam built on Narmada river in Gujarat", "The dam constructed on Satluj river").
  • For candidates with visual impairment, the same item is asked in words: "Name the dam built on …" or "Name the state where … dam is located".
  • Your textbook's map "India: Major Rivers and Dams" (p. 24) shows every one of these dams on its river. Study it alongside this page.

Note on spellings. The syllabus prints "Hirakund" and "Nagarjun Sagar"; your textbook prints Hirakud and Nagarjuna Sagar. Your textbook's text spells the river "Sutluj" and its map "Satluj"; papers often print "Sutlej". All of these name the same dam or river. Use your textbook's spelling.

  1. The eight dams

The map on this page and the trainer below use the same eight points, numbered north to south. The river of each dam is shown on your textbook's map; your textbook names a state only for Sardar Sarovar (Gujarat).

No. Dam River (your textbook's map)
1 Salal Chenab
2 Bhakra Nangal Satluj
3 Tehri upper Ganga
4 Rana Pratap Sagar Chambal
5 Sardar Sarovar Narmada
6 Hirakud Mahanadi
7 Nagarjuna Sagar Krishna
8 Tungabhadra Tungabhadra

Where to look on the outline map (the states, except Gujarat, are beyond your textbook):

  • Salal — the far north, in the hills of Jammu and Kashmir.
  • Bhakra Nangal — where Himachal Pradesh meets Punjab.
  • Tehri — the Himalayas of Uttarakhand.
  • Rana Pratap Sagar — the south-east of Rajasthan.
  • Sardar Sarovar — the south-east of Gujarat.
  • Hirakud — the west of Odisha.
  • Nagarjuna Sagar — the border of Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.
  • Tungabhadra — the east of Karnataka.
Beyond your textbook — the states

Your textbook gives only Sardar Sarovar's state (Gujarat). The others: Salal — Jammu and Kashmir; Bhakra Nangal — the Bhakra dam is in Himachal Pradesh and the Nangal dam, a little downstream, in Punjab; Tehri — Uttarakhand; Rana Pratap Sagar — Rajasthan; Hirakud — Odisha; Nagarjuna Sagar — on the border of Telangana and Andhra Pradesh; Tungabhadra — Karnataka. Board papers for visually impaired candidates have asked these states. Your textbook also links two of the rivers with their basins: Bhakra–Nangal is in the Sutluj-Beas river basin and Hirakud in the Mahanadi basin.

  1. Learn them in order

Memory hook — north to south: "S-B-T, R-S, H, N-T" — Salal, Bhakra Nangal, Tehri (the three northern dams) · Rana Pratap Sagar, Sardar Sarovar (the west) · Hirakud (the east) · Nagarjuna Sagar, Tungabhadra (the south).

Their rivers in the same order: "Chenab–Satluj–Ganga, Chambal–Narmada, Mahanadi, Krishna–Tungabhadra."

Two pairs that students mix up:

  • Rana Pratap Sagar and Gandhi Sagar. Both are on the Chambal on your textbook's map; Gandhi Sagar is just upstream (south) of Rana Pratap Sagar. Only Rana Pratap Sagar is in the syllabus list.
  • Nagarjuna Sagar and Tungabhadra. Nagarjuna Sagar is on the Krishna itself, further east; Tungabhadra is on its tributary, the Tungabhadra, further west and south.

  1. Reading or marking a point — three steps

  1. Which state or region? (far north hills, Himalayas, west, east, southern plateau)
  2. Which river? Use the table — every dam sits on its own river.
  3. Which dam? Write its name beside your symbol (●) on the map.

  1. Other dams on your textbook's map

Your textbook's map shows more dams than the syllabus list. Board papers have used some of them in matching and ordering questions, so know them by river: Gandhi Sagar and Kota Barrage (Chambal), Mettur (Kaveri), Koyna (near the head of the Krishna), Pravara (in the Godavari's upper basin), Rihand (Son basin), Tilaiya, Konar, Maithon and Panchet (Damodar valley), Naraura (Ganga) and Periyar (in the far south). They are not in the syllabus list for "locate and label".

Worked example 1

From description to dot

Question. On an outline map of India, locate and label: (a) the dam that stores the water of the Tungabhadra river; (b) the dam on the Chambal that is in the syllabus list; (c) the dam high on the Ganga, in the Himalayas. Say what you would mark and write for each.

Asked: three dams, where to mark them and what to write.

Concept used: The eight dams (section 2); two pairs that students mix up (section 3).

  1. 1

    (a) River Tungabhadra → Tungabhadra dam. Mark it in the east of Karnataka (beyond your textbook), on the Tungabhadra, west and south of Nagarjuna Sagar; write "Tungabhadra".

  2. 2

    (b) Chambal, syllabus list → Rana Pratap Sagar (not Gandhi Sagar). Mark it in the south-east of Rajasthan (beyond your textbook); write "Rana Pratap Sagar".

  3. 3

    (c) Upper Ganga in the Himalayas → Tehri. Mark it in the Himalayas of Uttarakhand (beyond your textbook); write "Tehri".

  4. Check: every answer is a different river, and each name comes from the syllabus list — so none of them can be another listed dam.

  5. Answer (a) Tungabhadra; (b) Rana Pratap Sagar; (c) Tehri — each marked with a dot and its name.

    Write it in the exam (3 marks):

    (a) ● Tungabhadra — marked on the Tungabhadra river. (1)
    (b) ● Rana Pratap Sagar — marked on the Chambal. (1)
    (c) ● Tehri — marked on the upper Ganga in the Himalayas. (1)

    Common trap: writing the river's name ("Chambal") instead of the dam's. The mark is for the dam.

Worked example 2

Which river?

Question. Name the river on which each dam stands, according to your textbook's map: (a) Nagarjuna Sagar; (b) Salal; (c) Hirakud; (d) Bhakra Nangal.

Asked: four rivers.

Concept used: The eight dams (section 2).

  1. 1

    (a) Nagarjuna Sagar → Krishna.

  2. 2

    (b) Salal → Chenab.

  3. 3

    (c) Hirakud → Mahanadi.

  4. 4

    (d) Bhakra Nangal → Satluj (your textbook: the "Sutluj-Beas river basin").

  5. Check: use the river hook in order — Chenab (Salal) and Satluj (Bhakra Nangal) are the first two; Mahanadi (Hirakud) is the east; Krishna (Nagarjuna Sagar) is in the south.

  6. Answer (a) Krishna; (b) Chenab; (c) Mahanadi; (d) Satluj.

    Common trap: giving the Beas for Bhakra Nangal because the textbook says "Sutluj-Beas basin". On the map, the dam is on the Satluj.

Worked example 3

North to south

Question. Arrange these dams from north to south: P. Tungabhadra; Q. Tehri; R. Sardar Sarovar; S. Rana Pratap Sagar.

Asked: an order from north to south.

Concept used: Learn them in order (section 3).

  1. 1

    Tehri (Q) — in the Himalayas; it is the only one of the four in the north.

  2. 2

    Rana Pratap Sagar (S) — on the Chambal, north-east of Sardar Sarovar.

  3. 3

    Sardar Sarovar (R) — in the south-east of Gujarat, further south than Rana Pratap Sagar.

  4. 4

    Tungabhadra (P) — on the southern plateau, west of Nagarjuna Sagar.

  5. Check: the hook "S-B-T, R-S, H, N-T" puts Tehri (T) before Rana Pratap Sagar (R), then Sardar Sarovar (S), and Tungabhadra (T) at the very end ✔.

  6. Answer Q, S, R, P

    Common trap: putting Sardar Sarovar before Rana Pratap Sagar because Gujarat is "further west". The question asks north to south, not west to east.

Common mistakes!

  • Wrong river: Hirakud on the Godavari, Salal on the Beas, Nagarjuna Sagar on the Kaveri, Tehri on the Yamuna.

    Your textbook's map: Salal — Chenab; Bhakra Nangal — Satluj; Tehri — upper Ganga; Rana Pratap Sagar — Chambal; Sardar Sarovar — Narmada; Hirakud — Mahanadi; Nagarjuna Sagar — Krishna; Tungabhadra — Tungabhadra.

    Learn the river hook in north-to-south order.

  • Wrong place: Salal marked in Punjab; Rana Pratap Sagar in Madhya Pradesh; Sardar Sarovar in Maharashtra; Nagarjuna Sagar and Tungabhadra swapped.

    Beyond your textbook: Salal is in the far north (Jammu and Kashmir); Rana Pratap Sagar in south-east Rajasthan (Gandhi Sagar is the one upstream); Sardar Sarovar in Gujarat; Nagarjuna Sagar is east of Tungabhadra.

    Place each dam by state, then river; practise with the trainer until you score 8 out of 8.

  • A dot without a name, or a name without a dot.

    "Locate and label" needs both: the symbol in the right place and the dam's name beside it.

    Draw the dot, then write the name on the map.

Key points

  • Syllabus map item: locate and label eight dams — Salal, Bhakra Nangal, Tehri, Rana Pratap Sagar, Sardar Sarovar, Hirakud, Nagarjuna Sagar, Tungabhadra.
  • Their rivers (your textbook's map), north to south: Chenab, Satluj, upper Ganga, Chambal, Narmada, Mahanadi, Krishna, Tungabhadra.
  • Your textbook names one state: Sardar Sarovar — Gujarat; it places Bhakra–Nangal in the Sutluj-Beas basin and Hirakud in the Mahanadi basin.
  • Board papers describe a dam by its river (and sometimes its state); visually impaired candidates are asked to name the dam or its state.
  • Locate and label = a symbol in the right place and the dam's name beside it.
