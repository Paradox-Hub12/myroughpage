Topic 7 of 7

Map work: the eight places

About 30 minutes · 3 things to learn
By the end you can…
  • Locate and label the Congress sessions of 1920 (Calcutta, Nagpur) and 1927 (Madras).
  • Locate and label the satyagrahas at Champaran, Kheda and Ahmedabad.
  • Locate and label Jallianwala Bagh (Amritsar) and Dandi, and link each of the eight places to its date and event.

Two marks in every board paper come from one small task: a dot on an outline map of India, and a line on which you write a name. The places never change — there are only eight of them for this chapter. Learn where they are, what happened there and when, and those two marks are yours.

Quick reminder (map reading)

On an outline map of India, north is at the top. The Arabian Sea is on the west (left), the Bay of Bengal on the east (right). Gujarat sticks out into the Arabian Sea on the west; Punjab is in the north-west, next to Pakistan; Bihar is in the east, along the border with Nepal; West Bengal reaches the Bay of Bengal in the east; Maharashtra is in the west-centre; Tamil Nadu is at the south-east tip.

  1. How map questions are asked

  • "Locate and label" — put a dot (or the symbol asked for) at the right place and write its name next to it.
  • "Identify" — a place is already marked A or B on the map; you read the clue, recognise the dot and write its name on the line.
  • For candidates with visual impairment, CBSE asks the same places as short-answer questions ("Name the place where…").
  • Always write the name neatly on or next to the line. Use the syllabus names (Calcutta, Madras); you may add today's names in brackets — (Kolkata), (Chennai).

  1. The eight places

Where they are

Place (syllabus name) State today Where on the map
1. Calcutta (Congress session) West Bengal east, near the Bay of Bengal, on the river Hooghly
2. Nagpur (Congress session) Maharashtra (east) almost the centre of India
3. Madras (Congress session) Tamil Nadu east coast, on the Bay of Bengal, in the south
4. Champaran (satyagraha) Bihar north-west Bihar, along the Nepal border
5. Kheda (satyagraha) Gujarat central Gujarat, a little south-east of Ahmedabad
6. Ahmedabad (mill workers' satyagraha) Gujarat central Gujarat, the northernmost of the three, on the Sabarmati river
7. Jallianwala Bagh Punjab in Amritsar, in the north-west, near the Pakistan border
8. Dandi (Dandi March) Gujarat south Gujarat coast, on the Arabian Sea, south of Surat

What happened there

Place When What happened (your textbook)
Calcutta (Congress session) September 1920 Gandhiji convinced the leaders to start non-cooperation for Khilafat and swaraj
Nagpur (Congress session) December 1920 the Non-Cooperation programme was adopted after a compromise
Madras (Congress session) 1927 a Congress session in the year Congress–League unity seemed possible (see the note below)
Champaran (satyagraha) 1917 peasants against the oppressive plantation system (indigo)
Kheda (satyagraha) 1918 peasants asked for revenue collection to be relaxed after crop failure and plague
Ahmedabad (mill workers' satyagraha) 1918 satyagraha amongst cotton mill workers
Jallianwala Bagh 13 April 1919 General Dyer fired on the crowd; hundreds killed
Dandi (Dandi March) reached on 6 April 1930 Gandhiji broke the salt law, making salt from sea water — the Civil Disobedience Movement began; the march started from his Sabarmati ashram (Ahmedabad)

Note on the Madras session (1927). Your textbook does not describe this session; the syllabus asks you only to locate it. Beyond your textbook: it was held in December 1927, and the Congress there resolved to boycott the Simon Commission (which arrived in 1928 — Topic 4). Learn its place for the map; do not write these extra details as textbook facts.

  1. Three places in Gujarat — telling them apart

Ahmedabad, Kheda and Dandi are all in Gujarat, so they sit close together:

  • Ahmedabad — the northernmost of the three, on the Sabarmati river (the Dandi March started from the Sabarmati ashram here).
  • Kheda — just south-east of Ahmedabad, inland, between Ahmedabad and the Gulf of Khambhat.
  • Dandi — far to the south, on the sea coast below Surat.

Hook: going south from Ahmedabad, you meet the farmers (Kheda) first and then the sea (Dandi) — "mill, field, sea".

  1. Linking map, date and event

Map questions often give a clue instead of a name: "the place where a massacre took place on 13 April 1919", "the place where Gandhiji led indigo peasants", "the city where the Congress met in September 1920". So learn each place as a triple: place — date — event (the table above). The map-points trainer below lets you practise exactly this.

Worked examples

Worked example 1

Identify from the clue

Question. Two places are described. Name each and say in which state it is today. (A) The town where the Congress session of December 1920 adopted the Non-Cooperation programme. (B) The place where Gandhiji supported peasants who could not pay land revenue after a plague epidemic.

Asked: the two places and their states.

Concept used: The place–date–event table (section 2).

  1. 1

    (A) December 1920 + programme adopted → Nagpur, in Maharashtra (near the centre of India).

  2. 2

    (B) revenue + plague → Kheda, in Gujarat (south-east of Ahmedabad).

  3. Check: the September 1920 session (Calcutta) only convinced the leaders, so a December "adopted" clue cannot be Calcutta; and the only revenue-and-plague satyagraha in the table is Kheda — Champaran's clue is plantations.

  4. Answer (A) Nagpur, Maharashtra. (B) Kheda, Gujarat.

    Common trap: writing "Calcutta" for (A) because "1920" fits both sessions. Look at the month and the verb.

Worked example 2

The Gujarat trio

Question. Three dots are marked in Gujarat on an outline map: P on the coast in the far south, Q the northernmost, on a river, and R a little south-east of Q, inland. Which dot is Dandi, which is Ahmedabad and which is Kheda?

Asked: match P, Q, R to the three places.

Concept used: Telling the Gujarat places apart (section 3).

  1. 1

    P — on the coast, far south → the salt was made from sea water, so it must be Dandi.

  2. 2

    Q — northernmost, on a river → Ahmedabad, on the Sabarmati.

  3. 3

    R — south-east of Q, inland → Kheda.

  4. Check: go south from Q: "mill (Ahmedabad) → field (Kheda) → sea (Dandi)" gives Q, R, P in order ✔. A coastal dot cannot be Kheda — Kheda's peasants were farming inland.

  5. Answer P = Dandi, Q = Ahmedabad, R = Kheda.

    Common trap: putting Dandi next to Ahmedabad because the march started there. The march started at Ahmedabad (Sabarmati ashram) and ended at Dandi on the coast, over 240 miles away.

Worked example 3

One place, one date, one event

Question. For each place, give the date (year is enough) and the event: Champaran, Calcutta, Dandi.

Asked: year and event for three places.

Concept used: Place–date–event triples (section 4).

  1. 1

    Champaran → 1917 — Gandhiji's satyagraha with peasants against the oppressive plantation system (Bihar).

  2. 2

    Calcutta → 1920 (September) — Congress session; Gandhiji convinced leaders to start non-cooperation for Khilafat and swaraj.

  3. 3

    Dandi → 1930 (6 April) — Gandhiji broke the salt law; the Civil Disobedience Movement began.

  4. Check: the three years are all different and in the order of Topics 1, 2 and 4 — Champaran first, Dandi last; if your Dandi year came before your Calcutta year, one of them is wrong.

  5. Answer Champaran — 1917, plantation satyagraha; Calcutta — September 1920, non-cooperation agreed; Dandi — 6 April 1930, salt law broken.

    Common trap: giving Dandi the year 1919 (confusing 6 April 1930 with the Rowlatt hartal of 6 April 1919).

Quick check · 3 of 3MCQ1 mark

On an outline map, a dot is placed on India's east coast in the south, on the Bay of Bengal. Which Congress session place of this chapter is it?

Madras (Chennai), Tamil Nadu — the 1927 session.
Nagpur is near the centre of India, far from the coast.
Calcutta is in the east but far to the north, in West Bengal.
Lahore is in Pakistan today and is not one of the eight map places.

Common mistakes!

  • Jallianwala Bagh marked in Delhi or Lahore.

    It is in Amritsar, Punjab.

    North-west, near the Pakistan border.

  • Champaran marked in Gujarat.

    Champaran is in Bihar, along the Nepal border.

    Only satyagraha outside Gujarat.

  • Ahmedabad, Kheda and Dandi swapped.

    They are close but different: north/river, inland south-east, southern coast.

    "Mill, field, sea" from north to south.

  • Madras session confused with the Justice Party.

    The Justice Party of Madras contested the 1920 elections; the map item is the 1927 Congress session at Madras.

    Madras on the map = the 1927 session.

  • Calcutta and Nagpur clues swapped.

    September 1920 = Calcutta (convinced); December 1920 = Nagpur (adopted).

    Read the month in the clue.

Key points

  • Eight places: Calcutta (Sept 1920), Nagpur (Dec 1920), Madras (1927) — Congress sessions; Champaran (1917, Bihar), Ahmedabad (1918, mill workers), Kheda (1918, revenue) — satyagrahas; Jallianwala Bagh (Amritsar, 13 April 1919); Dandi (6 April 1930).
  • Only Champaran of the three satyagrahas is outside Gujarat.
  • Gujarat from north to south: Ahmedabad → Kheda → Dandi ("mill, field, sea").
  • Learn each place as place — date — event; map clues usually give the date or event, not the name.
