Section A · what to look at again

Question 1 · MCQ

NCERT Fig. 3 shows soldiers in Amritsar in 1919 forcing people to crawl along a street. This picture belongs to the:

Correct: (a)

The "crawling orders" were part of the brutal repression after the Jallianwala Bagh massacre in Amritsar (April 1919). Options (b), (c) and (d) are events of 1928, 1922 and 1930.

Question 2 · MCQ

Arrange in chronological order, earliest first:
I. Gandhiji returns to India from South Africa
II. Chauri Chaura incident
III. A Khilafat Committee is formed in Bombay
IV. The Oudh Kisan Sabha is set up

Correct: (d)

I January 1915 → III March 1919 → IV October 1920 → II February 1922. Option (a) puts the Oudh Kisan Sabha before the Khilafat Committee; (b) puts the Khilafat Committee before Gandhiji's return; (c) puts Chauri Chaura before the Oudh Kisan Sabha.

Question 3 · MCQ

The Gudem Hills, where a guerrilla movement spread in the early 1920s, are in present-day:

Correct: (c)

The Gudem Hills are in Andhra Pradesh. Assam was the plantation workers' region, Bihar is Champaran's state and Gujarat has Kheda and Ahmedabad.

Question 4 · MCQ

Which statement about women in the Civil Disobedience Movement is correct?

Correct: (d)

The textbook says the increased public role did not necessarily mean any radical change in how women's position was visualised. (a) The Congress was reluctant to give women authority; (b) rural women came from rich peasant households; (c) they also made salt, marched and picketed liquor shops.

Question 5 · A–R
AAfter the Round Table Conference failed, business groups were no longer uniformly enthusiastic about the Civil Disobedience Movement.
RThey were worried about militant activities, a long disruption of business and the growing influence of socialism among younger Congress members.

Correct: (A)

Both are true, and R is the reason the textbook gives for business groups' falling enthusiasm.

Question 6 · A–R
AMahatma Gandhi called the "untouchables" harijan and organised satyagraha for their entry into temples.
RGandhiji believed that separate electorates would speed up the integration of dalits into society.

Correct: (C)

A is true. R is false: Gandhiji believed separate electorates would slow down dalits' integration.
