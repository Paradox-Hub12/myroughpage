Section A · what to look at again

Question 1 · MCQ

NCERT Fig. 4.3 is a photograph of a plantation. Of which crop, and in which region?

Correct: (b)

The caption of Fig. 4.3 reads "Bamboo plantation in North-east". Fig. 4.2 is the banana plantation in southern India; your textbook has no figure of a tea or rubber plantation by that number.

Question 2 · MCQ

In which year, according to your textbook, was India the second largest producer of cotton after China?

Correct: (c)

"In 2017, India was second largest producer of cotton after China." The year 2018 goes with tea, groundnut, rapeseed and fruits and vegetables.

Question 3 · MCQ

In 2018, India was the third largest producer of rapeseed in the world. Which two countries does your textbook place ahead of it?

Correct: (a)

"In rapeseed production India was third largest producer in the world after Canada and China in 2018." Brazil is ahead of India in sugarcane; Yemen is where Arabica coffee came from.

Question 4 · MCQ

Which tea-growing area of West Bengal does your textbook name?

Correct: (d)

The major tea-producing areas include the "hills of Darjeeling and Jalpaiguri districts, West Bengal". The Nilgiri and Baba Budan Hills are coffee areas; the Garo hills of Meghalaya are named for rubber.

Question 5 · A–R
AGroundnut accounts for about half of the major oilseeds produced in India.
RGroundnut is a rabi crop.

Correct: (C)

A is true: groundnut "accounts for about half of the major oilseeds produced in the country". R is false: groundnut is a kharif crop; linseed and mustard are the rabi oil seeds.

Question 6 · A–R
ATea is processed within the tea garden.
RProcessing it there restores its freshness.

Correct: (A)

Both are true: "Tea is processed within the tea garden to restore its freshness." R gives the reason for A.
