P25●●● AdvancedLong answer · 5 marksOriginal

Compare primitive subsistence farming and intensive subsistence farming in five points, using your textbook.

Full solution

Asked: five matched comparisons.

Concept used: Primitive and intensive side by side (Topic 1).

  1. 1

    Where: primitive — in a few pockets, on small patches; intensive — in areas of high population pressure on land.

  2. 2

    Inputs: primitive — no fertilisers or modern inputs; intensive — high doses of biochemical inputs and irrigation.

  3. 3

    Tools and labour: primitive — hoe, dao, digging sticks, family or community labour; intensive — labour-intensive.

  4. 4

    Land use: primitive — slash and burn, then shift when fertility falls (Nature replenishes it); intensive — the same small, uneconomical holdings farmed again and again (right of inheritance).

  5. 5

    Output: primitive — low land productivity; intensive — maximum output from limited land, with enormous pressure on it.

  6. Check: each point compares the same feature for both types — five features, five marks.

  7. Answer Primitive: small shifting patches, no modern inputs, simple tools, low productivity. Intensive: crowded areas, heavy inputs and irrigation, labour-intensive, maximum output from small inherited plots.

    Write it in the exam (5 marks):

    1. Primitive farming is done on small patches in a few pockets; intensive farming is done where population pressure on land is high. (1)
    2. Primitive farmers use no fertilisers or modern inputs; intensive farmers use high doses of biochemical inputs and irrigation. (1)
    3. Primitive farming uses hoe, dao and digging sticks with family labour; intensive farming is labour-intensive. (1)
    4. Primitive farmers slash, burn and shift to fresh patches; intensive farmers keep farming small holdings divided by inheritance. (1)
    5. Primitive farming has low land productivity; intensive farmers take maximum output from limited land. (1)
    Common trap: writing five points about one type only. "Compare" needs both in each point.
