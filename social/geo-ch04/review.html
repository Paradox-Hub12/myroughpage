Set 3 — Month 1

5 questions, about 12 minutes

Focus: whole-chapter questions, like the board exam.

M1.1 · 1 of 5●● Standard

Give two ways in which primitive subsistence farming and plantation farming are opposite, using your textbook. (2 marks)

Answer:

  1. Primitive farming grows food on small patches for the family; plantation grows a single crop on a large area for industry. (1) 2. Primitive farming uses no modern inputs; plantation uses capital intensive inputs and migrant labour. (1) (Topics 1–2)

Did you get it?
