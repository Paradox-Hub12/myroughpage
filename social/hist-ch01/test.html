Section A · what to look at again

Question 1 · MCQ

In the female allegories that artists used from the time of the French Revolution, how was Justice generally shown?

Correct: (b)

Justice was generally a blindfolded woman carrying a pair of weighing scales. The red cap and the broken chain stood for Liberty; the crown of oak leaves belongs to Germania.

Question 2 · MCQ

Arrange in chronological order, earliest first:
I. Mazzini is sent into exile after attempting a revolution in Liguria
II. The deputies of an all-German National Assembly take their seats in Frankfurt
III. The Grimm brothers publish their first collection of tales
IV. Garibaldi leads the Expedition of the Thousand

Correct: (c)

III 1812 → I 1831 → II 1848 → IV 1860. Option (a) puts the Grimms after Mazzini's exile; (b) puts the Expedition before Frankfurt; (d) puts the Grimms after Frankfurt.

Question 3 · MCQ

Which of these statements about Giuseppe Garibaldi are true?
I. He was a sailor in the merchant navy.
II. In 1867 his volunteers captured Rome from the French garrison.
III. In 1854 he supported Victor Emmanuel II in his efforts to unify the Italian states.
IV. His volunteers grew to about 30,000.

Correct: (d)

I, III and IV are from Box 2. II is false: in 1867 the Red Shirts were no match for the French and Papal troops; the Papal States joined Italy only in 1870.

Question 4 · MCQ

Which change did the autocratic monarchies of Central and Eastern Europe make in the years after 1848?

Correct: (a)

After 1848 monarchs granted concessions: serfdom and bonded labour were abolished in the Habsburg dominions and in Russia. Women were not given the vote; the treaty was not cancelled; censorship already existed after 1815.

Question 5 · A–R
AFrédéric Sorrieu's vision of a world of "democratic and social Republics" is described as utopian.
RIt shows a society so ideal that it is unlikely to actually exist.

Correct: (A)

Both are true, and R explains A: "utopian" means a vision of a society so ideal that it is unlikely to actually exist, which is what the print imagines.

Question 6 · A–R
ABritain became a nation-state through a sudden upheaval.
RBefore the eighteenth century there was no British nation; people saw themselves as English, Welsh, Scot or Irish.

Correct: (D)

A is false: Britain's nation-state was the result of a long-drawn-out process, not a sudden upheaval. R is true.

Question 7 · MCQ

Friedrich List, who wrote in 1834 about binding the Germans economically into a nation, was:

Correct: (a)

Source B introduces List as Professor of Economics at the University of Tübingen. Bismarck was Prussia's chief minister; Mazzini founded Young Europe; Wilhelm Wolff described the weavers.
