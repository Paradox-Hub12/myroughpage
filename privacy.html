Privacy

In short

You do not need an account, and we do not ask for your name, email or phone number. Your answers, scores and test results are not saved or sent anywhere — they stay on the page and are gone when you close it.

What is stored in your browser

Only one thing: your choice of light or dark mode, kept in your own browser so the site remembers it next time. You can clear it by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Other services

The site's fonts (Lexend and Kalam) are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers to fetch them, as it does for many websites. We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics, and we do not set cookies.

Changes

If we later add accounts or saved progress, this page will be updated first, and you will be told what is saved and why. Last updated: 20 November 2026.
