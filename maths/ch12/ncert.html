Exercise 12.1, Q1Two cubes joined end to end2 marks
  1. 2 cubes each of volume 64 cm³ are joined end to end. Find the surface area of the resulting cuboid.

π to use: none — this question has no circles.

Given: two cubes, each of volume 64 cm³, joined end to end. Asked: the surface area of the cuboid formed.

Concept used: edge of a cube =volume3= \sqrt[3]{\text{volume}}=3volume​; TSA of a cuboid =2(lb+bh+hl)= 2(lb + bh + hl)=2(lb+bh+hl) (Topic 1, Topic 2). Why it applies: two equal cubes side by side make a cuboid twice as long.

  1. Hidden faces: one face of each cube is pressed against the other — two faces of 16 cm² each.

  2. 1

    Edge: a3=64a^3 = 64a3=64, so a=4a = 4a=4 cm.

  3. 2

    The cuboid is 8×4×48 \times 4 \times 48×4×4 (cm).

  4. 3

    TSA =2(8×4+4×4+4×8)=2(32+16+32)=2×80=160= 2(8 \times 4 + 4 \times 4 + 4 \times 8) = 2(32 + 16 + 32) = 2 \times 80 = 160=2(8×4+4×4+4×8)=2(32+16+32)=2×80=160 cm².

  5. Check: two cubes have 2×6×16=1922 \times 6 \times 16 = 1922×6×16=192 cm²; the two hidden faces are 2×16=322 \times 16 = 322×16=32 cm²; 192−32=160192 - 32 = 160192−32=160 cm² ✔.

  6. Answer 160 cm²

    Common trap: Giving 192 cm² (the hidden faces counted) or 176 cm² (only one hidden face taken away).
Exercise 12.1, Q2The inner surface of a vessel3 marks
  1. A vessel is in the form of a hollow hemisphere mounted by a hollow cylinder. The diameter of the hemisphere is 14 cm and the total height of the vessel is 13 cm. Find the inner surface area of the vessel.

π to use: 227\dfrac{22}{7}722​ (the exercise's rule).

Given: hemisphere diameter 14 cm, so r=7r = 7r=7 cm; total height 13 cm. Asked: the inner surface area.

Concept used: inner surface = CSA of hemisphere + CSA of cylinder (Topic 3). Why it applies: the vessel is open at the top, and the two hollow parts open into each other.

  1. Hidden faces: there is no surface where the cylinder meets the hemisphere (the rim of the bowl and the bottom of the cylinder are joined and open), and none at the open top.

  2. 1

    Height of the cylinder =13−7=6= 13 - 7 = 6=13−7=6 cm.

  3. 2

    Inner CSA of hemisphere =2×227×7×7=308= 2 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 7 = 308=2×722​×7×7=308 cm².

  4. 3

    Inner CSA of cylinder =2×227×7×6=264= 2 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 6 = 264=2×722​×7×6=264 cm².

  5. 4

    Inner surface =308+264=572= 308 + 264 = 572=308+264=572 cm².

  6. Check: 2πr(h+r)=2×227×7×13=44×13=5722\pi r(h + r) = 2 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 13 = 44 \times 13 = 5722πr(h+r)=2×722​×7×13=44×13=572 cm² ✔.

  7. Answer 572 cm²

    Common trap: Taking the cylinder's height as 13 cm — the hemisphere's radius is part of the total height.
Exercise 12.1, Q3A toy: cone on a hemisphere3 marks
  1. A toy is in the form of a cone of radius 3.5 cm mounted on a hemisphere of same radius. The total height of the toy is 15.5 cm. Find the total surface area of the toy.

  2. 1

  3. 2

  4. 3

    CSA of cone =227×3.5×12.5=137.5= \dfrac{22}{7} \times 3.5 \times 12.5 = 137.5=722​×3.5×12.5=137.5 cm².

  5. 4

    CSA of hemisphere =2×227×3.5×3.5=77= 2 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 3.5 \times 3.5 = 77=2×722​×3.5×3.5=77 cm².

  6. 5

    TSA =137.5+77=214.5= 137.5 + 77 = 214.5=137.5+77=214.5 cm².

  7. Check: πr(l+2r)=227×3.5×(12.5+7)=11×19.5=214.5\pi r(l + 2r) = \dfrac{22}{7} \times 3.5 \times (12.5 + 7) = 11 \times 19.5 = 214.5πr(l+2r)=722​×3.5×(12.5+7)=11×19.5=214.5 cm² ✔.

  1. A hemispherical depression is cut out from one face of a cubical wooden block such that the diameter l of the hemisphere is equal to the edge of the cube. Determine the surface area of the remaining solid.

  2. 1

    TSA of cube =6l2= 6l^2=6l2.

  3. 2

  4. 3

    Inner surface =2π(l2)2=πl22= 2\pi \left(\dfrac{l}{2}\right)^2 = \dfrac{\pi l^2}{2}=2π(2l​)2=2πl2​.

  5. 4

    Surface =6l2−πl24+πl22=6l2+πl24=l24(π+24)= 6l^2 - \dfrac{\pi l^2}{4} + \dfrac{\pi l^2}{2} = 6l^2 + \dfrac{\pi l^2}{4} = \dfrac{l^2}{4}(\pi + 24)=6l2−4πl2​+2πl2​=6l2+4πl2​=4l2​(π+24).
