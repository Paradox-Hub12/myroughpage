This section currently holds the Surface Areas and Volumes items from the CBSE Mathematics Sample Question Papers 2026-27 and 2025-26 — Mathematics Standard (041) and Mathematics Basic (241) — answered with their official marking schemes (newest first). Past year board questions, and the "Exam focus" analysis, will be added once the papers are sourced.

Both 2026-27 sample papers state that there is no change in the question paper design and assessment pattern for 2026-27, so the 2025-26 items remain good practice. Standard and Basic share the same syllabus, so every student should try all nine items. This chapter is part of Unit VI, Mensuration (10 marks, shared with Areas Related to Circles). It carries 6 marks in the 2026-27 Standard paper (an MCQ and a 5-mark question with an internal choice), 6 marks in the 2026-27 Basic paper (an MCQ and a 5-mark question), 6 marks in the 2025-26 Standard paper (an MCQ and a 5-mark question with an internal choice) and 5 marks in the 2025-26 Basic paper (one 5-mark question). None of these items has an alternative for visually impaired candidates, and no case study or assertion–reason item is on this chapter. Every paper says "Take π=227\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}π=722​ wherever required, if not stated", so each item below uses 227\dfrac{22}{7}722​ unless its own text says otherwise; the value is also written under each question.

Each item has two parts:

  • Understand it — the full step-by-step explanation: Given / Asked → Concept used (and why) → Hidden faces → steps → Check → Common trap.
  • Write it in the exam — the answer as you should write it, following the official marking scheme, with marks shown. Where the scheme gives only a total for a group of lines, the split shown is inferred and marked so.
