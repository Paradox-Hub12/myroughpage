Halve a diameter first. Use the π the question gives (227\dfrac{22}{7}722​, 3.14 or "in terms of π"); round only at the end.

Surface area of joined solids — joined faces are hidden, so the TSA of a combination is not the sum of the TSAs. Routine: name the pieces → list the hidden faces → list the outside faces → word equation → numbers. Capsule 2πrh+4πr22\pi rh + 4\pi r^22πrh+4πr2; toy πrl+2πr2\pi rl + 2\pi r^2πrl+2πr2; tent 2πrh+πrl2\pi rh + \pi rl2πrh+πrl (no floor); hemisphere on a cube 6a2+πr26a^2 + \pi r^26a2+πr2; two cubes 10a210a^210a2. Unequal faces: two circles of the smaller radius are hidden, and the ring π(R2−r2)\pi(R^2 - r^2)π(R2−r2) stays outside. Read each part's height from the total: capsule L−2rL - 2rL−2r; toy H−rH - rH−r; tent H−hH - hH−h.

Surface area of cut-out solids — take away the opening, add the inside of the hollow. Hemisphere scooped from a face: +πr2+\pi r^2+πr2. Cone hollowed from a cylinder: 2πrh+πr2+πrl2\pi rh + \pi r^2 + \pi rl2πrh+πr2+πrl. Open vessels: inner curved surfaces only. Largest hemisphere, cone or cylinder in a cube of edge a: radius a2\dfrac{a}{2}2a​.

Volumes — joined: add (no space is lost). Hollow, hole, depression, raised bottom: subtract. Actual capacity = apparent − bump. Air in a shed = cuboid + half-cylinder. 1 litre = 1000 cm³; 1 m³ = 1000 litres; mass = volume × mass of 1 cm³.

Real life — a container that just fits has the solid's radius and full height (a sphere in a cube: edge 2r). Space or water left = container − solid; water that flows out = the solid. Number of spheres = volume pushed up ÷ one sphere. Work backwards by writing the volume with the unknown. Cost = (area + allowance) × rate. Not in the syllabus: melting and recasting, pouring from one vessel into another, and the frustum.
