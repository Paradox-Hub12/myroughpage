Question 2 · MCQ

A solid hemisphere of radius 5.5 cm is fixed on the 12 cm × 12 cm face of a cuboid 12 cm × 12 cm × 6 cm. Compared with the cuboid alone, the total surface area of the solid increases by: (Work in terms of π.)

Correct: (c)

Hidden faces: the hemisphere's flat face and the circle of the cuboid under it. The surface loses πr2=30.25π\pi r^2 = 30.25\piπr2=30.25π and gains 2πr2=60.5π2\pi r^2 = 60.5\pi2πr2=60.5π: a net increase of 30.25π30.25\pi30.25π cm².

Question 3 · MCQ

A tent is a cylinder of radius 20 m and height 3 m with a conical top of height 15 m. The area of canvas for the conical top is: (Use π = 3.14.)

Correct: (d)

Question 6 · A–R
AThe total surface area of a solid toy made of a cone of radius r and slant height l standing on a hemisphere of radius r is πrl+3πr2\pi r l + 3\pi r^2πrl+3πr2.
RThe total surface area of a solid hemisphere of radius r is 3πr23\pi r^23πr2.

Correct: (D)

R is true — a solid hemisphere has a curved part 2πr22\pi r^22πr2 and a flat face πr2\pi r^2πr2. A is false: in the toy the hemisphere's flat face is joined to the cone's base, so the TSA is πrl+2πr2\pi r l + 2\pi r^2πrl+2πr2, not πrl+3πr2\pi r l + 3\pi r^2πrl+3πr2.
