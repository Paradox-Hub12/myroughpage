Section A · what to look at again

Question 1 · MCQ

A point P is 34 cm from the centre of a circle of radius 16 cm. The length of the tangent from P to the circle is:

Correct: (b)

∠OTP=90°\angle OTP = 90°∠OTP=90° (tangent ⟂ radius), so PT=342−162=1156−256=900=30PT = \sqrt{34^2 - 16^2} = \sqrt{1156 - 256} = \sqrt{900} = 30PT=342−162​=1156−256​=900​=30 cm. (a) subtracts the lengths; (c) adds the squares, as if OP were a shorter side; (d) adds the lengths.

Question 2 · MCQ

PA and PB are tangents from P to a circle with centre O, and ∠APB=72°\angle APB = 72°∠APB=72°. Then ∠AOB\angle AOB∠AOB is:

Correct: (c)

OAPB has right angles at A and B, so ∠AOB=180°−72°=108°\angle AOB = 180° - 72° = 108°∠AOB=180°−72°=108°. (a) copies the angle at P; (b) halves it; (d) doubles it.

Question 3 · MCQ

A quadrilateral ABCD circumscribes a circle, with AB=10AB = 10AB=10 cm, BC=12BC = 12BC=12 cm and CD=15CD = 15CD=15 cm. Then DA is:

Correct: (b)

AB+CD=AD+BCAB + CD = AD + BCAB+CD=AD+BC: 10+15=DA+1210 + 15 = DA + 1210+15=DA+12, so DA=13DA = 13DA=13 cm. (a) AB+BC−CDAB + BC - CDAB+BC−CD and (c) BC+CD−ABBC + CD - ABBC+CD−AB pair adjacent sides; (d) adds the three sides.

Question 4 · MCQ

Two concentric circles have radii 39 cm and 36 cm. The length of the chord of the larger circle which touches the smaller circle is:

Correct: (b)

The chord is bisected at the point of contact: half-chord =392−362=225=15= \sqrt{39^2 - 36^2} = \sqrt{225} = 15=392−362​=225​=15 cm, chord =30= 30=30 cm. (a) is only half the chord; (c) adds the radii; (d) adds the squares.

Question 5 · MCQ

Two tangents to a circle of radius 7 cm are inclined to each other at 60°. The length of each tangent is:

Correct: (d)

OP bisects the 60° angle, so ∠OPA=30°\angle OPA = 30°∠OPA=30° and tan⁡30°=7PA\tan 30° = \dfrac{7}{PA}tan30°=PA7​: PA=73PA = 7\sqrt{3}PA=73​ cm. (a) is the radius; (b) is OP (=2r= 2r=2r); (c) uses tan⁡60°\tan 60°tan60°, the whole angle.

Question 6 · A–R
ATwo tangents can be drawn to a circle from a point inside it.
RA tangent meets a circle in exactly one point.

Correct: (D)

A is false: every line through a point inside a circle cuts it in two points, so no tangent passes through an inside point. R is true — it is the meaning of a tangent.
