Day 1

Set 1 — Day 1

5 questions, about 8 minutes

Focus: the words, the counts and the two theorems.

D1.1 · 1 of 5● Foundation

What is the point of contact of a tangent?

Answer: The one point the tangent has in common with the circle. (Topic 2)

Did you get it?

D1.2 · 2 of 5● Foundation

At most how many tangents to a circle can be parallel to a given line, and where do they touch?

Answer: Two; they touch at the two ends of a diameter. (Topics 2, 3)

Did you get it?

D1.3 · 3 of 5● Foundation

In the proof of Theorem 10.1, why must every other point Q of the tangent lie outside the circle?

Answer: If Q were inside, the line would cut the circle in two points and be a secant, not a tangent. (Topic 3)

Did you get it?

D1.4 · 4 of 5● Foundation

The tangent from P to a circle of radius 12 cm is 35 cm. How far is P from the centre?

Answer: 122+352=1369=37\sqrt{12^2 + 35^2} = \sqrt{1369} = 37122+352​=1369​=37 cm — OP is the hypotenuse. (Topic 3)

Did you get it?

D1.5 · 5 of 5● Foundation

State Theorem 10.2 and the construction its proof needs.

Answer: The lengths of the tangents drawn from an external point to a circle are equal. Join the centre to the external point and to both points of contact. (Topic 4)

M1.1 · 1 of 5●● Standard

Prove Theorem 10.2 by Pythagoras' theorem (not by congruence).

Answer: Tangents PQ, PR; join OP, OQ, OR. ∠OQP=∠ORP=90°\angle OQP = \angle ORP = 90°∠OQP=∠ORP=90° (tangent ⟂ radius). PQ2=OP2−OQ2PQ^2 = OP^2 - OQ^2PQ2=OP2−OQ2 and PR2=OP2−OR2PR^2 = OP^2 - OR^2PR2=OP2−OR2; OQ=OROQ = OROQ=OR (radii), so PQ2=PR2PQ^2 = PR^2PQ2=PR2 and PQ=PRPQ = PRPQ=PR. (Topic 4)

Did you get it?

M1.2 · 2 of 5●● Standard

Write the proof of Theorem 10.1 in three or four lines with reasons.

Answer: XY touches the circle at P; take any other point Q of XY and join OQ. Q is outside the circle (else XY would be a secant), so OQ>OPOQ > OPOQ>OP. This holds for every such Q, so OP is the shortest segment from O to XY. The shortest segment from a point to a line is perpendicular to it, so OP⊥XYOP \perp XYOP⊥XY. (Topic 3)

Did you get it?

M1.3 · 3 of 5●● Standard

From A, the tangents AP and AQ to a circle are 14 cm each. A tangent at a point of the minor arc PQ meets AP at B and AQ at C. Find the perimeter of △ABC.

Answer: AP+AQ=28AP + AQ = 28AP+AQ=28 cm (BC's two pieces equal BP and CQ). (Topic 5)
