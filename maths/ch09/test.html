Section A · what to look at again

Question 1 · MCQ

From a point on level ground 19 m from the foot of a vertical tower, the angle of elevation of the top of the tower is 60°. The height of the tower is:

Correct: (b)

tan⁡60°=h19\tan 60° = \dfrac{h}{19}tan60°=19h​, so h=193h = 19\sqrt{3}h=193​ m. (a) uses tan⁡30°\tan 30°tan30° instead of tan⁡60°\tan 60°tan60° (193=1933\dfrac{19}{\sqrt{3}} = \dfrac{19\sqrt{3}}{3}3​19​=3193​​); (c) would need 45°; (d) uses sin⁡60°\sin 60°sin60°, as if 19 m were the line of sight.

Question 2 · MCQ

A man at the top of a lighthouse looks at a boat. The angle between his line of sight and the vertical lighthouse is 30°. The angle of depression of the boat is:

Correct: (b)

The angle of depression is measured from the horizontal, which is at 90° to the lighthouse: 90°−30°=60°90° - 30° = 60°90°−30°=60°. (a) measures from the vertical; (c) is the angle between the vertical and the horizontal; (d) is not an angle of depression at all.

Question 3 · MCQ

A tight rope 22 m long is tied from the top of a vertical pole to a point on the ground and makes an angle of 60° with the ground. The height of the pole is:

Correct: (b)

The rope is the hypotenuse: sin⁡60°=h22\sin 60° = \dfrac{h}{22}sin60°=22h​, so h=22×32=113h = 22 \times \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = 11\sqrt{3}h=22×23​​=113​ m. (a) uses tan, taller than the rope — impossible; (c) uses sin⁡30°\sin 30°sin30° or cos⁡60°\cos 60°cos60° (that is the distance along the ground); (d) uses tan⁡30°\tan 30°tan30°: 223=2233\dfrac{22}{\sqrt{3}} = \dfrac{22\sqrt{3}}{3}3​22​=3223​​.

Question 4 · MCQ

A boy whose eyes are 1.2 m above the ground stands 15 m from a vertical pole. The angle of elevation of the top of the pole from his eyes is 45°. The height of the pole is:

Correct: (c)

Above the eyes: 15tan⁡45°=1515\tan 45° = 1515tan45°=15 m; add the eye height: 15+1.2=16.215 + 1.2 = 16.215+1.2=16.2 m. (a) forgets the eye height; (b) subtracts it; (d) uses tan⁡60°\tan 60°tan60°.

Question 5 · MCQ

A vertical pole 838\sqrt{3}83​ m high casts a shadow 8 m long on level ground. The Sun's altitude is:

Correct: (c)

tan⁡θ=838=3\tan \theta = \dfrac{8\sqrt{3}}{8} = \sqrt{3}tanθ=883​​=3​, so θ=60°\theta = 60°θ=60°. (a) turns the ratio upside down (883=13\dfrac{8}{8\sqrt{3}} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}83​8​=3​1​); (b) needs the shadow equal to the pole; (d) means no shadow.

Question 6 · A–R
AThe angle of elevation of the top of a tower 26 m high, from a point on level ground 26326\sqrt{3}263​ m from its foot, is 60°.

A is false: tan⁡θ=26263=13\tan \theta = \dfrac{26}{26\sqrt{3}} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}tanθ=263​26​=3​1​, so θ = 30°, not 60°. R is true: it is the definition of tan in this right triangle.
