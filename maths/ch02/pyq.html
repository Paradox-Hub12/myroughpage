Question: Assertion (A): If zeroes of the polynomial (2k−1)x2+4x−3(2k - 1)x^2 + 4x - 3(2k−1)x2+4x−3 are reciprocal of each other, then k=−1k = -1k=−1.

Given: an assertion and a reason about reciprocal zeroes. Asked: which option is correct.

Concept used: product of zeroes =ca= \dfrac{c}{a}=ac​; reciprocals multiply to 1 (Topic 5). Why it applies: both statements are about the product.

  1. 1

    R: if a=ca = ca=c, the product of the zeroes is ca=1\dfrac{c}{a} = 1ac​=1; two numbers with product 1 are reciprocals. True (whenever the polynomial has zeroes).

  2. 2

    A: reciprocal zeroes need a=ca = ca=c: 2k−1=−32k - 1 = -32k−1=−3.

  3. 3

    Add 1 to both sides: 2k=−22k = -22k=−2, so k=−1k = -1k=−1. True.

  4. 4

    A uses R in the other direction (reciprocal zeroes ⇔ product 1 ⇔ a = c). CBSE's key reads R as this full condition, so R explains A → option A.

    Common trap: Setting 2k−1=32k - 1 = 32k−1=3 (dropping the minus sign of c=−3c = -3c=−3), which gives k=2k = 2k=2.
