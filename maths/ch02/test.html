Section A · what to look at again

Question 1 · MCQ

The graph of a quadratic polynomial p(x)p(x)p(x) is a parabola opening downwards whose highest point is (1,−2)(1, -2)(1,−2). The number of zeroes of p(x)p(x)p(x) is:

Correct: (a)

A downward parabola whose highest point is below the x-axis never reaches the axis: no zeroes. (b) counts the turning point as a zero; (c) assumes every parabola has two zeroes; (d) gives the y-coordinate of the highest point.
