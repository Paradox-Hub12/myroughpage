Question 1 · MCQ

The pair of equations 3x−4y=53x - 4y = 53x−4y=5 and 9x−12y=109x - 12y = 109x−12y=10 has:

Correct: (a)

Ratios 39=13\dfrac{3}{9} = \dfrac{1}{3}93​=31​, −4−12=13\dfrac{-4}{-12} = \dfrac{1}{3}−12−4​=31​, 510=12\dfrac{5}{10} = \dfrac{1}{2}105​=21​: equal, equal, not equal → parallel → no solution. (b) needs all three equal; (c) needs the first two different; (d) never happens for two lines.

Question 2 · MCQ

The pair 2x+3y=72x + 3y = 72x+3y=7 and 4x+ky=144x + ky = 144x+ky=14 has infinitely many solutions when k is:

Correct: (a)

24=3k=714\dfrac{2}{4} = \dfrac{3}{k} = \dfrac{7}{14}42​=k3​=147​ gives k=6k = 6k=6. (b) turns the ratio upside down (k3=12\dfrac{k}{3} = \dfrac{1}{2}3k​=21​); (c) is a sign slip; (d) that is the form of the answer for a unique solution.

Question 3 · MCQ

The area of the triangle formed by the lines x+y=6x + y = 6x+y=6, x−y=2x - y = 2x−y=2 and the x-axis is:

Correct: (a)

The lines meet at (4,2)(4, 2)(4,2) and cut the x-axis at (6,0)(6, 0)(6,0) and (2,0)(2, 0)(2,0): base 4, height 2, area 12×4×2=4\dfrac{1}{2} \times 4 \times 2 = 421​×4×2=4. (b) leaves out the 12\dfrac{1}{2}21​; (c) uses the slanting side 222\sqrt{2}22​ (from (2,0)(2, 0)(2,0) to (4,2)(4, 2)(4,2)) as the height; (d) is the triangle with the y-axis.

Question 4 · MCQ

A two-digit number has tens digit a and units digit b. The number exceeds the number formed by reversing its digits by 36. The equation for this is:

Correct: (a)

The number is 10a+b10a + b10a+b and its reverse is 10b+a10b + a10b+a; "exceeds by 36" means number − reverse =36= 36=36. (b) uses digit sums, which are equal; (c) has the subtraction the wrong way round; (d) multiplies instead of subtracting.

Question 5 · MCQ

While solving a pair of linear equations by substitution (into the other equation), a student arrives at the statement 7=77 = 77=7. The pair has:

Correct: (a)

A true statement with no variable means every solution of one equation satisfies the other: coincident lines. (b) would need a false statement; (c) reads the 7 as a value of x; (d) would need a value of x or y.
