A two-digit number is 4 times the sum of its digits. If 27 is added to the number, its digits are reversed. Find the number.

Answer: 10x+y=4(x+y)10x + y = 4(x + y)10x+y=4(x+y) gives y=2xy = 2xy=2x; 10x+y+27=10y+x10x + y + 27 = 10y + x10x+y+27=10y+x gives x−y=−3x - y = -3x−y=−3. So x=3x = 3x=3, y=6y = 6y=6: the number is 36. Check: 4×9=364 \times 9 = 364×9=36 ✔, 36+27=6336 + 27 = 6336+27=63 ✔. (Topic 6)
