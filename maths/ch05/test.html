Question 1 · MCQ

The 20th term of the AP 9,16,23,…9, 16, 23, \ldots9,16,23,… is:

Correct: (b)

a20=9+19×7=142a_{20} = 9 + 19 \times 7 = 142a20​=9+19×7=142. (a) is a+nd=9+20×7a + nd = 9 + 20 \times 7a+nd=9+20×7, the 21st term; (c) is n×dn \times dn×d only; (d) uses d=9−16=−7d = 9 - 16 = -7d=9−16=−7.

Question 3 · MCQ

Which term of the AP −2,2,6,…-2, 2, 6, \ldots−2,2,6,… is 78?

Correct: (a)

78=−2+4(n−1)78 = -2 + 4(n - 1)78=−2+4(n−1), so n−1=20n - 1 = 20n−1=20 and n=21n = 21n=21. (b) stops at n−1n - 1n−1; (c) takes d=2d = 2d=2 (the second term) instead of 2−(−2)=42 - (-2) = 42−(−2)=4: −2+40×2=78-2 + 40 \times 2 = 78−2+40×2=78; (d) is the difference 78−(−2)78 - (-2)78−(−2), a value, not a position.

Question 4 · MCQ

If k−1k - 1k−1, k+3k + 3k+3 and 3k−13k - 13k−1 are three consecutive terms of an AP, then k is:

Correct: (d)

2(k+3)=(k−1)+(3k−1)2(k + 3) = (k - 1) + (3k - 1)2(k+3)=(k−1)+(3k−1) gives 2k+6=4k−22k + 6 = 4k - 22k+6=4k−2, so k=4k = 4k=4 (terms 3, 7, 11). (a) comes from putting the first term in the middle, 2(k−1)=(k+3)+(3k−1)2(k - 1) = (k + 3) + (3k - 1)2(k−1)=(k+3)+(3k−1); (b) from b=a+cb = a + cb=a+c (missing the 2); (c) is the first term at k=4k = 4k=4, not k.
