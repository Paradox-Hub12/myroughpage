Chapter 5 · Unit II

Arithmetic Progressions

5 topicsAbout 7½ hoursBoard exam
Why this chapter matters

A salary that rises by the same amount every year, savings that grow by a fixed sum each week, rows of seats that each have two more than the row in front: all of these are arithmetic progressions — lists that add the same number at every step. Once you spot that pattern, two short formulas let you jump straight to any term (the 100th row, the 30th instalment) and add up any number of terms (all the seats, the whole loan) without writing the list out.

In the board exam this chapter is part of Unit II, Algebra (20 marks, shared with Polynomials, Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables and Quadratic Equations), in both Mathematics Standard and Mathematics Basic. In the 2026-27 sample papers it supplies a 1-mark item and a 3-mark question in Standard (4 marks), and two 1-mark items and a 4-mark case study in Basic (6 marks).
