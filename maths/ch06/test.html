In △ABC, DE∥BCDE \parallel BCDE∥BC with D on AB and E on AC. If AD=3AD = 3AD=3 cm, DB=4.5DB = 4.5DB=4.5 cm and AE=2.4AE = 2.4AE=2.4 cm, then EC is:

Question 2 · MCQ

In △PQR, ∠P=35°\angle P = 35°∠P=35° and ∠Q=85°\angle Q = 85°∠Q=85°. In △KLM, ∠K=60°\angle K = 60°∠K=60° and ∠M=85°\angle M = 85°∠M=85°. Then:

Correct: (b)

∠R=60°\angle R = 60°∠R=60° and ∠L=35°\angle L = 35°∠L=35°; so P↔LP \leftrightarrow LP↔L (35°), Q↔MQ \leftrightarrow MQ↔M (85°), R↔KR \leftrightarrow KR↔K (60°). (a) pairs 35° with 60°; (c) pairs 35° with 85°; (d) pairs 85° with 60°.

Question 3 · MCQ

Question 6 · A–R
AIf in △ABC and △PQR, ∠A=∠P\angle A = \angle P∠A=∠P and ABPQ=BCQR\dfrac{AB}{PQ} = \dfrac{BC}{QR}PQAB​=QRBC​, then △ABC ~ △PQR.
RIf one angle of a triangle is equal to one angle of another triangle and the sides including these angles are proportional, then the two triangles are similar.

Correct: (D)

A is false: AB and BC meet at B, so the equal angles needed for SAS are ∠B\angle B∠B and ∠Q\angle Q∠Q, not ∠A\angle A∠A and ∠P\angle P∠P — the triangles need not be similar. R is the SAS criterion — true — but it does not support A, because A's angle is not included.
