The whole chapter on one page

Similar figures — same shape, not necessarily the same size. All circles, all squares and all equilateral triangles are similar. Congruent figures are similar (scale factor 1), but similar figures need not be congruent.

Similar polygons (same number of sides) — corresponding angles equal and corresponding sides proportional; both are needed (square vs rectangle; square vs rhombus). The common ratio of corresponding sides is the scale factor.

Basic Proportionality Theorem (BPT) — "If a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle to intersect the other two sides in distinct points, the other two sides are divided in the same ratio": in △ABC with DE∥BCDE \parallel BCDE∥BC, ADDB=AEEC\dfrac{AD}{DB} = \dfrac{AE}{EC}DBAD​=ECAE​ (also ADAB=AEAC\dfrac{AD}{AB} = \dfrac{AE}{AC}ABAD​=ACAE​). Proof (by areas): join BE and CD; draw DM⊥ACDM \perp ACDM⊥AC, EN⊥ABEN \perp ABEN⊥AB; ar(ADE)ar(BDE)=ADDB\dfrac{\text{ar}(ADE)}{\text{ar}(BDE)} = \dfrac{AD}{DB}ar(BDE)ar(ADE)​=DBAD​, ar(ADE)ar(DEC)=AEEC\dfrac{\text{ar}(ADE)}{\text{ar}(DEC)} = \dfrac{AE}{EC}ar(DEC)ar(ADE)​=ECAE​, and ar(BDE) = ar(DEC) because they are on the same base DE and between the same parallels DE and BC.

Converse of BPT — "If a line divides any two sides of a triangle in the same ratio, then the line is parallel to the third side." Unequal ratios → not parallel. Never assume parallel from a sketch.

Similar triangles — △ABC ~ △DEF means A↔DA \leftrightarrow DA↔D, B↔EB \leftrightarrow EB↔E, C↔FC \leftrightarrow FC↔F: corresponding angles equal and ABDE=BCEF=CAFD\dfrac{AB}{DE} = \dfrac{BC}{EF} = \dfrac{CA}{FD}DEAB​=EFBC​=FDCA​. Criteria (stated without proof): AA (two pairs of equal angles), SSS (three side ratios equal), SAS (one equal angle and the two sides forming it in one ratio). An angle that is not between the two sides gives no criterion.

Applications — shadows (each height with its own shadow); lamp-posts (big base = distance + shadow); perimeters, medians and altitudes of similar triangles are in the side ratio (not squared); trapezium diagonals: OAOC=OBOD=ABCD\dfrac{OA}{OC} = \dfrac{OB}{OD} = \dfrac{AB}{CD}OCOA​=ODOB​=CDAB​; right triangle with BD⊥ACBD \perp ACBD⊥AC: △ADB ~ △ABC ~ △BDC.

Not for the exam: the RHS similarity criterion (NCERT's note to the reader). Not in the 2026-27 syllabus: areas of similar triangles; proofs of Pythagoras' theorem.
