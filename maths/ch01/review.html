M1.1 · 1 of 5●● Standard

Prove that 3\sqrt{3}3​ is irrational.

Answer: Suppose 3=ab\sqrt{3} = \dfrac{a}{b}3​=ba​, a and b co-prime integers, b≠0b \neq 0b=0. Then 3b2=a23b^2 = a^23b2=a2, so 3 divides a2a^2a2 and (3 prime) 3 divides a. Write a=3ca = 3ca=3c: 3b2=9c23b^2 = 9c^23b2=9c2, so b2=3c2b^2 = 3c^2b2=3c2 and 3 divides b. Then 3 divides both a and b — contradicting co-prime. Hence 3\sqrt{3}3​ is irrational. (Topic 5)

Did you get it?
