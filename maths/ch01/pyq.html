Question: The greatest number which divides both 134 and 188, leaving remainders 4 and 6 respectively, is:

Given: 134 leaves remainder 4; 188 leaves remainder 6. Asked: the greatest such divisor.

Concept used: subtract the remainders, then take the HCF (Topic 4). Why it applies: the divisor goes exactly into 134−4134 - 4134−4 and 188−6188 - 6188−6, and must be as large as possible.

  1. 1

    Subtract the remainders.

    134−4=130,188−6=182134 - 4 = 130, \qquad 188 - 6 = 182134−4=130,188−6=182Swipe to see the whole equation →
  2. 2

    Factorise.

    130=2×5×13,182=2×7×13130 = 2 \times 5 \times 13, \qquad 182 = 2 \times 7 \times 13130=2×5×13,182=2×7×13Swipe to see the whole equation →
  3. 3

    HCF: common primes 2 and 13.

    HCF(130,182)=2×13=26\text{HCF}(130, 182) = 2 \times 13 = 26HCF(130,182)=2×13=26Swipe to see the whole equation →
  4. 4

    26 is bigger than both remainders, so it is valid → option B.

  5. Check: 134=26×5+4134 = 26 \times 5 + 4134=26×5+4 ✔; 188=26×7+6188 = 26 \times 7 + 6188=26×7+6 ✔.

    Why the other options are wrong: A — 13 works, but it is not the greatest. C — 39 does not divide 130. D — 65 does not divide 182.
    Common trap: Taking HCF(134, 188) = 2 without subtracting the remainders.
(B) 26. 134−4=130134 - 4 = 130134−4=130 and 188−6=182188 - 6 = 182188−6=182; HCF(130, 182) = 26

Question: There are three bells placed at different swings in a park, which toll at intervals of 5, 6 and 8 minutes, respectively. They all toll together when the park opens for the visitors at 10:00 a.m.. How many more times do they all toll together till the park is closed at 7:00 p.m.?

Given: intervals 5, 6, 8 minutes; together at 10:00 a.m.; park closes at 7:00 p.m. Asked: how many more times they toll together.

Concept used: LCM, then count (Topic 4). Why it applies: the bells line up only at common multiples of 5, 6 and 8 minutes.

  3. 3

    Count: 4 more times.

  5. Check: 10 a.m. to 7 p.m. is 9 hours = 540 minutes; 540=120×4+60540 = 120 \times 4 + 60540=120×4+60, so 4 complete intervals ✔.

  6. Answer 4 more times

    Common trap: Counting the 10:00 a.m. toll as well and answering 5. The question asks how many more times.
