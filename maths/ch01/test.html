Section A · what to look at again

Question 1 · MCQ

The prime factorisation of 1 848 is:

Correct: (a)

1848=23×3×7×111848 = 2^3 \times 3 \times 7 \times 111848=23×3×7×11 (8×2318 \times 2318×231, and 231=3×7×11231 = 3 \times 7 \times 11231=3×7×11). (b) miscounts the 2s (22×3×7×11=9242^2 \times 3 \times 7 \times 11 = 92422×3×7×11=924); (c) and (d) contain composite factors (8, 21).

Question 2 · MCQ

The LCM of 22×33×52^2 \times 3^3 \times 522×33×5 and 23×3×72^3 \times 3 \times 723×3×7 is:

Correct: (b)

LCM = largest power of every prime: 23×33×5×72^3 \times 3^3 \times 5 \times 723×33×5×7. (a) is the HCF; (c) leaves out the primes that are not common; (d) uses the smallest powers.

Question 3 · MCQ

The HCF of two numbers is 6 and their LCM is 180. If one of the numbers is 36, the other is:

Correct: (a)

6×180=36×x6 \times 180 = 36 \times x6×180=36×x, so x=108036=30x = \dfrac{1080}{36} = 30x=361080​=30. (b) stops at HCF × LCM; (c) divides the LCM by 36; (d) multiplies 36 by the HCF.

Question 4 · MCQ

Which of the following is irrational?

Correct: (d)

4−64 - \sqrt{6}4−6​ is rational − irrational, so irrational. (a) =3= 3=3; (b) =36=6= \sqrt{36} = 6=36​=6; (c) =1.5= 1.5=1.5 — all rational.

Question 5 · MCQ

Two signal lights flash every 18 seconds and every 24 seconds. They flash together at 8:00:00 p.m. They will next flash together at:

Correct: (b)

LCM(18, 24) =23×32=72= 2^3 \times 3^2 = 72=23×32=72 s = 1 min 12 s, so 8:01:12 p.m. (a) uses the HCF (6 s); (c) uses the product 18×24=43218 \times 24 = 43218×24=432 s; (d) adds the intervals.

Question 6 · A–R
A9n9^n9n cannot end with the digit 0 for any natural number n.
RA number ends with the digit 0 only if its prime factorisation contains both 2 and 5.

Correct: (A)

9n=32n9^n = 3^{2n}9n=32n has no 2 and no 5, so A is true; R is true and is exactly the reason.
