n=40n = 40n=40, n2=20\dfrac{n}{2} = 202n​=20; cf: 4, 14, 21, 36, 40 — the first cf greater than 20 is 21, for 20 – 30.

50+18−1236−12−9×10=50+615×10=5450 + \dfrac{18 - 12}{36 - 12 - 9} \times 10 = 50 + \dfrac{6}{15} \times 10 = 5450+36−12−918−12​×10=50+156​×10=54.

3 Median=52.6+2×56.2=1653\,\text{Median} = 52.6 + 2 \times 56.2 = 1653Median=52.6+2×56.2=165, so Median =55= 55=55.
