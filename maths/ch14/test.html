Section A · what to look at again

Question 1 · MCQ

A die is rolled once. The probability that the number shown is a multiple of 2 or a multiple of 3 is:

Correct: (a)

Multiples of 2: 2, 4, 6; of 3: 3, 6; 6 is in both. Favourable 2, 3, 4, 6: 46=23\dfrac{4}{6} = \dfrac{2}{3}64​=32​.

Question 2 · MCQ

One card is picked from a well-shuffled pack of 52 playing cards. The probability that it is a diamond but not a king, queen or jack is:

Correct: (c)

Diamonds that are not face cards: ace and 2 to 10 = 10 cards. 1052=526\dfrac{10}{52} = \dfrac{5}{26}5210​=265​.

Question 3 · MCQ

A coin is flipped three times. The probability that the second and third flips both land tails is:

Correct: (d)

Of the 8 outcomes, HTT and TTT end in two tails: 28=14\dfrac{2}{8} = \dfrac{1}{4}82​=41​.

Question 4 · MCQ

A library shelf holds 80 books. For a book taken at random, the probability that it is a storybook is 0.4. How many storybooks are on the shelf?

Correct: (a)

Storybooks =0.4×80=32= 0.4 \times 80 = 32=0.4×80=32.

Question 5 · MCQ

Two dice are rolled. The probability that they show different numbers whose sum is even is:

Correct: (b)

Even sums: both odd (3×3=93 \times 3 = 93×3=9) or both even (3×3=93 \times 3 = 93×3=9) = 18; take away the 6 doubles: 12. 1236=13\dfrac{12}{36} = \dfrac{1}{3}3612​=31​.

Question 6 · A–R
AThe probability that a non-leap year picked at random has 53 Wednesdays is 27\dfrac{2}{7}72​.
RA non-leap year has 365 days, that is, 52 weeks and 1 day.

Correct: (D)

A: 365 days = 52 weeks + 1 day; the extra day is a Wednesday in 1 case out of 7, so the probability is 17\dfrac{1}{7}71​, not 27\dfrac{2}{7}72​ — false. R: true (52×7+1=36552 \times 7 + 1 = 36552×7+1=365). (27\dfrac{2}{7}72​ is the answer for a leap year.)
