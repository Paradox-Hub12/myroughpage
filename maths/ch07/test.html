Question 1 · MCQ

The distance between the points (−1,3)(-1, 3)(−1,3) and (4,−9)(4, -9)(4,−9) is:

Correct: (b)

(4−(−1))2+(−9−3)2=25+144=169=13\sqrt{(4 - (-1))^2 + (-9 - 3)^2} = \sqrt{25 + 144} = \sqrt{169} = 13(4−(−1))2+(−9−3)2​=25+144​=169​=13. (a) takes −9−3-9 - 3−9−3 as −6-6−6: 25+36=61\sqrt{25 + 36} = \sqrt{61}25+36​=61​; (c) leaves out the root; (d) takes the points the other way round and writes (−1−4)2=(−5)2(-1 - 4)^2 = (-5)^2(−1−4)2=(−5)2 as −25-25−25: −25+144=119\sqrt{-25 + 144} = \sqrt{119}−25+144​=119​: −25+144=119\sqrt{-25 + 144} = \sqrt{119}−25+144​=119​.

Question 2 · MCQ

The point which divides the segment joining A(2, −3) and B(−8, 7) internally in the ratio 2 : 3 is:

Correct: (a)

x=2×(−8)+3×25=−105=−2x = \dfrac{2 \times (-8) + 3 \times 2}{5} = \dfrac{-10}{5} = -2x=52×(−8)+3×2​=5−10​=−2, y=2×7+3×(−3)5=55=1y = \dfrac{2 \times 7 + 3 \times (-3)}{5} = \dfrac{5}{5} = 1y=52×7+3×(−3)​=55​=1. (b) is the 3 : 2 point (2 multiplied by A's coordinates); (c) is the mid-point; (d) forgets to divide by 2+32 + 32+3.

Question 3 · MCQ

The distance of the point (−7,−2)(-7, -2)(−7,−2) from the x-axis is:

Correct: (c)

The perpendicular to the x-axis has length ∣y∣=∣−2∣=2\lvert y \rvert = \lvert -2 \rvert = 2∣y∣=∣−2∣=2. (a) is the distance from the y-axis; (b) a distance is never negative; (d) is the distance from the origin, 49+4\sqrt{49 + 4}49+4​.

Question 4 · MCQ

The points A(−1, 1), B(1, 4), C(3, 1) and D(1, −2), taken in order, are the vertices of:

Correct: (d)

AB=BC=CD=DA=13AB = BC = CD = DA = \sqrt{13}AB=BC=CD=DA=13​, but AC=4AC = 4AC=4 and BD=6BD = 6BD=6: four equal sides, unequal diagonals. (a) needs equal diagonals; (b) needs equal diagonals; (c) is wrong because all four sides are equal.

Question 5 · MCQ

If the distance between the points (x,4)(x, 4)(x,4) and (1,1)(1, 1)(1,1) is 5 units, then:

Correct: (c)

(x−1)2+9=25(x - 1)^2 + 9 = 25(x−1)2+9=25, so (x−1)2=16(x - 1)^2 = 16(x−1)2=16 and x−1=±4x - 1 = \pm 4x−1=±4: x=5x = 5x=5 or x=−3x = -3x=−3. (a) and (b) keep only one value; (d) solves x2=16x^2 = 16x2=16, forgetting the −1-1−1.
