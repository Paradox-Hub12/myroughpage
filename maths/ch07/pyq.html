Sample paper and case-study questions

16 questions from official CBSE sources. Each answer has two views: Understand it (every step) and Write it in the exam (what earns the marks).

Past board papers not yet available — to be added. This section currently holds the Coordinate Geometry items from the CBSE Mathematics Sample Question Papers 2026-27 and 2025-26 — Mathematics Standard (041) and Mathematics Basic (241) — answered with their official marking schemes (newest first). Past year board questions, and the "Exam focus" analysis, will be added once the papers are sourced.

Both 2026-27 sample papers state that there is no change in the question paper design and assessment pattern for 2026-27, so the 2025-26 items remain good practice. Standard and Basic share the same syllabus, so every student should try all sixteen items. In each paper this chapter is Unit III, Coordinate Geometry, worth 6 marks, and each of the four papers gives it exactly 6 marks: three 1-mark items and a 3-mark item in the 2026-27 Standard paper; a 1-mark, a 2-mark and a 3-mark item in the 2026-27 Basic paper; two 1-mark items and a 4-mark case study in each 2025-26 paper. The 2025-26 Basic paper also uses coordinates in a 2-mark circle question (Q22, Unit IV) and in its alternative for visually impaired candidates; both are included. The 2025-26 Standard case study (Q37) has an alternative for visually impaired candidates that gives the same three points by their coordinates instead of in a picture; it shares the entry below.

Each item has two parts:

  • Understand it — the full step-by-step explanation: Given / Asked → Concept used (and why) → steps → Check → answer → Common trap.
  • Write it in the exam — the answer as you should write it, following the official marking scheme, with marks shown. Where the scheme gives only a total for a group of lines, the split shown is inferred and marked so.
