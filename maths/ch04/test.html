Section A · what to look at again

Question 1 · MCQ

Which of the following is not a quadratic equation?

Correct: (b)

x2+4x=x2−8x^2 + 4x = x^2 - 8x2+4x=x2−8 gives 4x+8=04x + 8 = 04x+8=0 — linear. (a) gives x2−8x+8=0x^2 - 8x + 8 = 0x2−8x+8=0; (c) gives x2+3x−2=0x^2 + 3x - 2 = 0x2+3x−2=0; (d) gives 2x2+3x+1=02x^2 + 3x + 1 = 02x2+3x+1=0 after the cubes cancel — all quadratic.

Question 2 · MCQ

The roots of 2x2=7x−32x^2 = 7x - 32x2=7x−3 are:

Correct: (a)

Standard form 2x2−7x+3=02x^2 - 7x + 3 = 02x2−7x+3=0 → (2x−1)(x−3)=0(2x - 1)(x - 3) = 0(2x−1)(x−3)=0 → x=12,3x = \dfrac{1}{2}, 3x=21​,3. (b) uses −b=−7-b = -7−b=−7 (wrong sign of bbb); (c) divides by a=2a = 2a=2 instead of 2a=42a = 42a=4 in the formula, 7±52\dfrac{7 \pm 5}{2}27±5​; (d) swaps aaa and ccc (the roots of 3x2−7x+2=03x^2 - 7x + 2 = 03x2−7x+2=0).

Question 3 · MCQ

If x2+kx+9=0x^2 + kx + 9 = 0x2+kx+9=0 has two equal roots, then kkk is:

Correct: (b)

k2−36=0k^2 - 36 = 0k2−36=0 → k=±6k = \pm 6k=±6. (a) misses the negative root; (c) uses 4ac=94ac = 94ac=9 (forgets the 4); (d) gives k2k^2k2, not kkk.

Question 4 · MCQ

The product of two consecutive positive odd integers is 195. If the smaller integer is xxx, the equation is:

Correct: (a)

Consecutive odd integers are xxx and x+2x + 2x+2: x(x+2)=195x(x + 2) = 195x(x+2)=195 → x2+2x−195=0x^2 + 2x - 195 = 0x2+2x−195=0 (roots 13 and −15-15−15; integers 13 and 15). (b) uses consecutive integers; (c) has the wrong sign of ccc; (d) adds instead of multiplying.

Question 5 · MCQ

If x=−2x = -2x=−2 is a root of 3x2+kx−2=03x^2 + kx - 2 = 03x2+kx−2=0, then kkk is:

Correct: (a)

3(4)−2k−2=03(4) - 2k - 2 = 03(4)−2k−2=0 → 10−2k=010 - 2k = 010−2k=0 → k=5k = 5k=5. (b) sign slip in k(−2)k(-2)k(−2); (c) takes ccc as +2+2+2; (d) squares −2-2−2 as −4-4−4.
