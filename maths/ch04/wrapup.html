Wrap-up

Which method?

About 35 minutes

In the exam, nobody tells you which method to use. This page gives you a flowchart for choosing in a few seconds, then works through five mixed problems that need you to choose.

The "Which method?" flowchart

  1. Is it in standard form? If not, expand, bring every term to the left and collect like terms. Read aaa, bbb, ccc with signs.
  2. Fractions in the equation? Multiply every term by the LCM of the denominators (note any values of xxx that make a denominator zero — they can't be roots).
  3. Common factor? Divide every term by it.
  4. Is c=0c = 0c=0? Take xxx out: x(ax+b)=0x(ax + b) = 0x(ax+b)=0, so x=0x = 0x=0 or x=−bax = -\dfrac{b}{a}x=−ab​. Never divide by xxx.
  5. Is b=0b = 0b=0? Then ax2=−cax^2 = -cax2=−c: find x2x^2x2 and take the square root — both signs.
  6. Can you spot a split within about 30 seconds? Factorise.
  7. Otherwise: find D=b2−4acD = b^2 - 4acD=b2−4ac.
    - D<0D < 0D<0 → no real roots. Stop.
    - D=0D = 0D=0 → x=−b2ax = -\dfrac{b}{2a}x=−2ab​, twice.
    - D>0D > 0D>0 → quadratic formula; simplify any surd.
  8. Check by substituting, and in a word problem reject roots that don't fit.

The question decides too: "by factorisation" means factorise; "by the quadratic formula" means the formula; "find the nature of the roots" needs only DDD.

Mixed example 1

No middle term

Question. Solve 3x2−75=03x^2 - 75 = 03x2−75=0.

Given: a=3a = 3a=3, b=0b = 0b=0, c=−75c = -75c=−75. Asked: the roots.

Concept used: when b=0b = 0b=0, isolate x2x^2x2 and take square roots. Why it applies: there is no xxx term, so splitting or the formula would be slower.

  1. 1

    Add 75 to both sides.

    3x2=753x^2 = 753x2=75Swipe to see the whole equation →
  2. 2

    Divide both sides by 3.

    x2=25x^2 = 25x2=25Swipe to see the whole equation →
  3. 3

    Take the square root — both signs.

    x=5orx=−5x = 5 \quad\text{or}\quad x = -5x=5orx=−5Swipe to see the whole equation →

  4. Check: 3(25)−75=03(25) - 75 = 03(25)−75=0 for both ✔.

  5. Answer x=±5x = \pm 5x=±5

    Common trap: Writing only x=5x = 5x=5. (−5)2=25(-5)^2 = 25(−5)2=25 as well.

Mixed example 2

An equation with fractions

Question. Solve 1x−1x+3=118\dfrac{1}{x} - \dfrac{1}{x + 3} = \dfrac{1}{18}x1​−x+31​=181​, where x≠0,−3x \neq 0, -3x=0,−3.

Given: an equation with xxx in denominators. Asked: the roots.

Concept used: combine the fractions, cross-multiply to get a quadratic, then factorise. Why it applies: after clearing denominators the equation is quadratic.

  1. 1

    Combine the left side over the common denominator x(x+3)x(x + 3)x(x+3).

    (x+3)−xx(x+3)=118\dfrac{(x + 3) - x}{x(x + 3)} = \dfrac{1}{18}x(x+3)(x+3)−x​=181​Swipe to see the whole equation →
  2. 2

    Simplify the top.

    3x(x+3)=118\dfrac{3}{x(x + 3)} = \dfrac{1}{18}x(x+3)3​=181​Swipe to see the whole equation →
  3. 3

    Cross-multiply.

    54=x(x+3)54 = x(x + 3)54=x(x+3)Swipe to see the whole equation →
  4. 4

    Expand the right side.

    54=x2+3x54 = x^2 + 3x54=x2+3xSwipe to see the whole equation →
  5. 5

    Subtract 54 from both sides.

    0=x2+3x−54, i.e. x2+3x−54=00 = x^2 + 3x - 54, \text{ i.e. } x^2 + 3x - 54 = 00=x2+3x−54, i.e. x2+3x−54=0Swipe to see the whole equation →
  6. 6

    Product −54-54−54, sum 3 → 999 and −6-6−6.

    (x+9)(x−6)=0(x + 9)(x - 6) = 0(x+9)(x−6)=0Swipe to see the whole equation →
  7. 7

    So x=6x = 6x=6 or x=−9x = -9x=−9. Neither is 0 or −3-3−3, so both are allowed.

  8. Check: x=6x = 6x=6: 16−19=3−218=118\dfrac{1}{6} - \dfrac{1}{9} = \dfrac{3 - 2}{18} = \dfrac{1}{18}61​−91​=183−2​=181​ ✔. x=−9x = -9x=−9: −19+16=118-\dfrac{1}{9} + \dfrac{1}{6} = \dfrac{1}{18}−91​+61​=181​ ✔.

  9. Answer x=6 or x=−9x = 6 \text{ or } x = -9x=6 or x=−9

    Common trap: "Cross-multiplying" each fraction separately, e.g. writing x−(x+3)=18x - (x + 3) = 18x−(x+3)=18. Combine the left side into a single fraction first.

Mixed example 3

A number and its reciprocal

Question. Solve x+1x=2512x + \dfrac{1}{x} = \dfrac{25}{12}x+x1​=1225​, x≠0x \neq 0x=0.

Given: a sum of xxx and its reciprocal. Asked: the roots.

Concept used: multiply through by the LCM 12x12x12x; then the formula. Why it applies: clearing fractions gives a quadratic with large coefficients — a split is not obvious.

  1. 1

    Multiply every term by 12x12x12x.

    12x2+12=25x12x^2 + 12 = 25x12x2+12=25xSwipe to see the whole equation →
  2. 2

    Standard form.

    12x2−25x+12=012x^2 - 25x + 12 = 012x2−25x+12=0Swipe to see the whole equation →
  3. 3

    D=(−25)2−4(12)(12)=625−576=49D = (-25)^2 - 4(12)(12) = 625 - 576 = 49D=(−25)2−4(12)(12)=625−576=49, so D=7\sqrt{D} = 7D​=7.

  4. 4

    Formula; −b=+25-b = +25−b=+25, 2a=242a = 242a=24.

    x=25±724x = \dfrac{25 \pm 7}{24}x=2425±7​Swipe to see the whole equation →
  5. 5

    The two roots.

    x=3224=43orx=1824=34x = \dfrac{32}{24} = \dfrac{4}{3} \quad\text{or}\quad x = \dfrac{18}{24} = \dfrac{3}{4}x=2432​=34​orx=2418​=43​Swipe to see the whole equation →

  6. Check: 43+34=16+912=2512\dfrac{4}{3} + \dfrac{3}{4} = \dfrac{16 + 9}{12} = \dfrac{25}{12}34​+43​=1216+9​=1225​ ✔ (the two roots are reciprocals of each other, which makes sense).

  7. Answer x=43 or x=34x = \dfrac{4}{3} \text{ or } x = \dfrac{3}{4}x=34​ or x=43​

    Common trap: Multiplying only the fractions by 12x12x12x and leaving the plain xxx alone. Every term gets multiplied.

Mixed example 4

Work and time (two taps)

Question. Two taps together fill a tank in 6 hours. The larger tap alone fills it in 5 hours less than the smaller tap alone. How long does each tap take by itself?

Given: together 6 h; larger tap 5 h faster. Asked: each tap's time.

Concept used: a tap that fills a tank in ttt hours fills 1t\dfrac{1}{t}t1​ of it per hour; rates add. Why it applies: the combined rate is 16\dfrac{1}{6}61​ per hour.

  1. 1

    Let the smaller tap take xxx hours. The larger tap takes (x−5)(x - 5)(x−5) hours.

  2. 2

    In one hour they fill 1x\dfrac{1}{x}x1​ and 1x−5\dfrac{1}{x - 5}x−51​ of the tank; together, 16\dfrac{1}{6}61​.

    1x+1x−5=16\dfrac{1}{x} + \dfrac{1}{x - 5} = \dfrac{1}{6}x1​+x−51​=61​Swipe to see the whole equation →
  3. 3

    Combine the left side.

    (x−5)+xx(x−5)=16\dfrac{(x - 5) + x}{x(x - 5)} = \dfrac{1}{6}x(x−5)(x−5)+x​=61​Swipe to see the whole equation →
  4. 4

    Cross-multiply.

    6(2x−5)=x(x−5)6(2x - 5) = x(x - 5)6(2x−5)=x(x−5)Swipe to see the whole equation →
  5. 5

    Expand both sides.

    12x−30=x2−5x12x - 30 = x^2 - 5x12x−30=x2−5xSwipe to see the whole equation →
  6. 6

    Standard form.

    x2−17x+30=0x^2 - 17x + 30 = 0x2−17x+30=0Swipe to see the whole equation →
  7. 7

    Product 30, sum −17-17−17 → −15-15−15 and −2-2−2.

    (x−15)(x−2)=0(x - 15)(x - 2) = 0(x−15)(x−2)=0Swipe to see the whole equation →
  8. 8

    So x=15x = 15x=15 or x=2x = 2x=2.

  9. 9

    Reject x=2x = 2x=2: the larger tap would take 2−5=−32 - 5 = -32−5=−3 hours — impossible.

  10. Check: 115+110=2+330=16\dfrac{1}{15} + \dfrac{1}{10} = \dfrac{2 + 3}{30} = \dfrac{1}{6}151​+101​=302+3​=61​ ✔.

  11. Answer The smaller tap takes 15 hours and the larger tap 10 hours.

    Common trap: Adding the times (x+(x−5)=6x + (x - 5) = 6x+(x−5)=6). Times do not add; the amounts filled per hour do.

Mixed example 5

Find p, then solve

Question. For what values of ppp does x2+px+16=0x^2 + px + 16 = 0x2+px+16=0 have two equal roots? Find the roots for each value.

Given: a=1a = 1a=1, b=pb = pb=p, c=16c = 16c=16. Asked: ppp and the roots.

Concept used: equal roots ⇔ D=0D = 0D=0; then x=−b2ax = -\dfrac{b}{2a}x=−2ab​. Why it applies: a kkk-type question followed by solving.

  1. 1

    Equal roots.

    p2−4(1)(16)=0p^2 - 4(1)(16) = 0p2−4(1)(16)=0Swipe to see the whole equation →
  2. 2

    Simplify.

    p2=64p^2 = 64p2=64Swipe to see the whole equation →
  3. 3

    Both square roots.

    p=8orp=−8p = 8 \quad\text{or}\quad p = -8p=8orp=−8Swipe to see the whole equation →
  4. 4

    a=1≠0a = 1 \neq 0a=1=0, so both are valid.

  5. 5

    For p=8p = 8p=8: x=−82=−4x = -\dfrac{8}{2} = -4x=−28​=−4. For p=−8p = -8p=−8: x=82=4x = \dfrac{8}{2} = 4x=28​=4.

  6. Check: x2+8x+16=(x+4)2x^2 + 8x + 16 = (x + 4)^2x2+8x+16=(x+4)2 ✔; x2−8x+16=(x−4)2x^2 - 8x + 16 = (x - 4)^2x2−8x+16=(x−4)2 ✔.

  7. Answer p=±8p = \pm 8p=±8; roots −4,−4-4, -4−4,−4 when p=8p = 8p=8 and 4,44, 44,4 when p=−8p = -8p=−8.

    Common trap: Mixing up the pairs — with p=8p = 8p=8 the root is −4-4−4, not +4+4+4 (x=−b2ax = -\dfrac{b}{2a}x=−2ab​).
