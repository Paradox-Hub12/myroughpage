Exercise 11.1, Q1A 60° sector2 marks
  1. Find the area of a sector of a circle with radius 6 cm if angle of the sector is 60°.

Given: r=6r = 6r=6 cm; θ=60°\theta = 60°θ=60°; π=227\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}π=722​. Asked: the area of the sector.

Concept used: area of a sector =θ360×πr2= \dfrac{\theta}{360} \times \pi r^2=360θ​×πr2 (Topic 2). Why it applies: a sector is the fraction θ360\dfrac{\theta}{360}360θ​ of the circle.

  1. 1

    Fraction: 60360=16\dfrac{60}{360} = \dfrac{1}{6}36060​=61​.

  2. 2

    Area =16×227×6×6=22×67=1327= \dfrac{1}{6} \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 6 \times 6 = \dfrac{22 \times 6}{7} = \dfrac{132}{7}=61​×722​×6×6=722×6​=7132​ cm².

  3. 3

    1327≈18.86\dfrac{132}{7} \approx 18.867132​≈18.86 cm².

  4. Check: the whole circle is 227×36=7927\dfrac{22}{7} \times 36 = \dfrac{792}{7}722​×36=7792​ cm², and 7927÷6=1327\dfrac{792}{7} \div 6 = \dfrac{132}{7}7792​÷6=7132​ ✔.

  5. Answer 1327\dfrac{132}{7}7132​ cm² (about 18.86 cm²)

    Common trap: Using 60180\dfrac{60}{180}18060​, which gives twice the answer. A full turn is 360°.
