Question 1 · MCQ

The area of a circle is 12 474 cm². Its circumference is: (Use π=227\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}π=722​.)

Correct: (b)

227r2=12 474\dfrac{22}{7} r^2 = 12\,474722​r2=12474, so r2=3969r^2 = 3969r2=3969 and r=63r = 63r=63 cm; circumference =2×227×63=396= 2 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 63 = 396=2×722​×63=396 cm.

Question 2 · MCQ

The length of the arc of a sector of radius 4.2 cm and angle 150° is: (Use π=227\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}π=722​.)

Correct: (c)

l=150360×2×227×4.2=512×26.4=11l = \dfrac{150}{360} \times 2 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 4.2 = \dfrac{5}{12} \times 26.4 = 11l=360150​×2×722​×4.2=125​×26.4=11 cm.

Question 3 · MCQ

A chord AB of a circle with centre O and radius 22 cm subtends 60° at the centre. The area of △OAB is:

Correct: (d)

At 60°, △OAB is equilateral with side 22 cm: area =34×222=1213= \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \times 22^2 = 121\sqrt{3}=43​​×222=1213​ cm².

Question 4 · MCQ

A horse is tied at a corner of a square field of side 50 m with a rope 42 m long. The area it can graze is: (Use π=227\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}π=722​.)

Correct: (c)

The corner of a square is 90°, and 42<5042 < 5042<50: 14×227×42×42=1386\dfrac{1}{4} \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 42 \times 42 = 138641​×722​×42×42=1386 m².

Question 5 · MCQ

A chord of a circle of radius 38.5 cm subtends 90° at the centre, and the minor segment has area 423.5 cm². The area of the major segment is: (Use π=227\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}π=722​.)

Correct: (a)

Major segment = circle − minor segment =227×38.52−423.5=4658.5−423.5=4235= \dfrac{22}{7} \times 38.5^2 - 423.5 = 4658.5 - 423.5 = 4235=722​×38.52−423.5=4658.5−423.5=4235 cm².

Question 6 · A–R
AThe perimeter of a sector of radius 9 cm and angle 80° is 4π4\pi4π cm.
RThe length of the arc of a sector of angle θ (in degrees) of a circle of radius r is θ360×2πr\dfrac{\theta}{360} \times 2\pi r360θ​×2πr.

Correct: (D)

R is true — it is the arc-length formula. A is false: 4π4\pi4π cm is only the arc (80360×2π×9=4π\dfrac{80}{360} \times 2\pi \times 9 = 4\pi36080​×2π×9=4π); the perimeter also has the two radii, so it is (4π+18)(4\pi + 18)(4π+18) cm.
