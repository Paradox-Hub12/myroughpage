Topic 1 of 5 · Bridge lesson

Toolkit: circles, triangles and π

About 30 minutes · 3 things to learn
By the end you can…
  • Find the circumference and area of a circle from its radius or diameter, and the radius from a circumference or an area, with π=227\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}π=722​, π = 3.14 or in terms of π.
  • Find the area of a triangle (half base × height; a right triangle; an equilateral triangle) and use sin and cos of 30°, 45°, 60° for a height.
  • Solve ring and wheel problems (area of a ring; distance in one revolution = circumference) with correct units.
Why this lesson exists

Every question in this chapter ends with the area or circumference of a circle, the area of a triangle, or a calculation with π. When an answer goes wrong, the slip is very often here — a diameter used as a radius, a triangle's height guessed, or π=227\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}π=722​ mixed with π = 3.14. If you already have these skills, prove it below and go straight to Topic 2.

Cut a round roti with a knife from the middle to the edge, twice — the piece you take out is a sector. Cut it once straight across, away from the middle — the smaller piece is a segment. This chapter is about finding the areas and the edges of such pieces. Every one of those answers is "a part of the whole circle", sometimes minus a triangle. So before the new ideas, make sure the whole circle and the triangle are ready.

  1. Circle words and the two circle formulas

A circle has a centre O and a radius r. The diameter goes right across through O: diameter =2×= 2 \times=2× radius. The distance round the circle is its circumference (its perimeter).

circumference=2πrarea=πr2\text{circumference} = 2\pi r \qquad \text{area} = \pi r^2circumference=2πrarea=πr2Swipe to see the whole equation →
  • Read the question twice: "diameter 42 cm" means r=21r = 21r=21 cm. Halve a diameter before you use a formula.
  • Units: a circumference is a length (cm, m); an area is in square units (cm², m²).

  1. Which π?

π is the same number for every circle: about 3.14159…. It cannot be written exactly as a fraction or a decimal, so a question tells you what to use.

The question says Use Typical radii
"Use π=227\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}π=722​" (or nothing — CBSE papers then say take 227\dfrac{22}{7}722​) 227\dfrac{22}{7}722​ multiples of 7 (7, 14, 21, 3.5…)
"Use π=3.14\pi = 3.14π=3.14" 3.14 5, 10, 20…
"in terms of π" keep π as a letter: 49π49\pi49π cm² any

Use one value of π in one answer, and round only at the very end.

If you know the circumference C, then r=C2πr = \dfrac{C}{2\pi}r=2πC​. If you know the area A, then r2=Aπr^2 = \dfrac{A}{\pi}r2=πA​; then take the square root.

  1. Rings and wheels

  • A ring (the path round a pond, a washer) lies between two circles with the same centre, radii R (outer) and r (inner):
ring=πR2−πr2=π(R2−r2)\text{ring} = \pi R^2 - \pi r^2 = \pi (R^2 - r^2)ring=πR2−πr2=π(R2−r2)Swipe to see the whole equation →

  1. Angle sums

The angles of a triangle add up to 180°; the angles of a quadrilateral add up to 360°; the angles round a point add up to 360°. You will use these in Topic 5.

Worked example 1

From the circumference to the area

Question. A circular garden has a circumference of 220 m. Find its radius and its area. (Use π=227\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}π=722​.)

Given: circumference 220 m; π=227\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}π=722​. Asked: r and the area.

Concept used: circumference =2πr= 2\pi r=2πr, worked backwards; then area =πr2= \pi r^2=πr2. Why it applies: the circumference is a known length of the same circle.

  1. 1

    Write the formula with the given value:

    2×227×r=2202 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times r = 2202×722​×r=220Swipe to see the whole equation →
  2. 2

    Simplify the left side: 447r=220\dfrac{44}{7} r = 220744​r=220.

  3. 3

    Multiply both sides by 744\dfrac{7}{44}447​: r=220×744=35r = 220 \times \dfrac{7}{44} = 35r=220×447​=35 m.

  4. 4

    Area:

    πr2=227×35×35=22×5×35=3850 m2\pi r^2 = \dfrac{22}{7} \times 35 \times 35 = 22 \times 5 \times 35 = 3850 \text{ m}^2πr2=722​×35×35=22×5×35=3850 m2Swipe to see the whole equation →

  5. Check: 2×227×35=2202 \times \dfrac{22}{7} \times 35 = 2202×722​×35=220 ✔.

  6. Answer radius 35 m; area 3850 m²

    Common trap: Dividing 220 by 227\dfrac{22}{7}722​ only (that gives the diameter, 70 m) and calling it the radius.

Worked example 2

The path round a pool (a ring)

Question. A circular pool has radius 14 m. A path 7 m wide runs all round it. Find the area of the path. (Use π=227\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}π=722​.)

Given: inner radius r=14r = 14r=14 m; width 7 m. Asked: the area of the path.

Concept used: area of a ring =π(R2−r2)= \pi(R^2 - r^2)=π(R2−r2). Why it applies: the path lies between two circles with the same centre.

  1. 1

    Outer radius: R=14+7=21R = 14 + 7 = 21R=14+7=21 m.

  2. 2

    Area of the path:

    π(R2−r2)=227×(212−142)\pi(R^2 - r^2) = \dfrac{22}{7} \times (21^2 - 14^2)π(R2−r2)=722​×(212−142)Swipe to see the whole equation →
  3. 3

    212−142=441−196=24521^2 - 14^2 = 441 - 196 = 245212−142=441−196=245.

  4. 4

    227×245=22×35=770\dfrac{22}{7} \times 245 = 22 \times 35 = 770722​×245=22×35=770 m².

  5. Check: big circle 227×441=1386\dfrac{22}{7} \times 441 = 1386722​×441=1386 m², pool 227×196=616\dfrac{22}{7} \times 196 = 616722​×196=616 m²; 1386−616=7701386 - 616 = 7701386−616=770 ✔.

  6. Answer 770 m²

    Common trap: Using π×72\pi \times 7^2π×72 — the width is not a radius. Or π(21−14)2\pi(21 - 14)^2π(21−14)2: square each radius first, then subtract.

Worked example 3

How many turns does the wheel make?

Question. A cycle wheel has diameter 63 cm. How many complete turns does it make in covering 3.96 km? (Use π=227\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}π=722​.)

Given: diameter 63 cm; distance 3.96 km. Asked: the number of turns.

Concept used: in one turn the wheel moves one circumference; turns =distancecircumference= \dfrac{\text{distance}}{\text{circumference}}=circumferencedistance​ in the same unit. Why it applies: the wheel rolls without slipping.

  1. 1

    Circumference =π×diameter=227×63=198= \pi \times \text{diameter} = \dfrac{22}{7} \times 63 = 198=π×diameter=722​×63=198 cm.

  2. 2

    Distance in cm: 3.96 km=3.96×100 000=396 0003.96 \text{ km} = 3.96 \times 100\,000 = 396\,0003.96 km=3.96×100000=396000 cm.

  3. 3

    Number of turns:

    396 000198=2000\dfrac{396\,000}{198} = 2000198396000​=2000Swipe to see the whole equation →

  4. Check: 2000×198=396 0002000 \times 198 = 396\,0002000×198=396000 cm =3.96= 3.96=3.96 km ✔.

Worked example 4

Two triangles you will meet again

Question. Find the area of (a) a right triangle whose two shorter sides are 9 cm each, and (b) an equilateral triangle of side 8 cm. (Use 3=1.73\sqrt{3} = 1.733​=1.73.)

Given: (a) legs 9 cm and 9 cm; (b) side 8 cm. Asked: the two areas.

Concept used: area =12×= \dfrac{1}{2} \times=21​× base ×\times× height; for an equilateral triangle, 34a2\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}a^243​​a2. Why it applies: in (a) the legs are at right angles; in (b) all sides are equal.

  1. 1

    (a) The legs are the base and the height: area =12×9×9=40.5= \dfrac{1}{2} \times 9 \times 9 = 40.5=21​×9×9=40.5 cm².

  2. 2

    (b) Height =8×sin⁡60°=8×32=43= 8 \times \sin 60° = 8 \times \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = 4\sqrt{3}=8×sin60°=8×23​​=43​ cm.

    Area =12×8×43=163= \dfrac{1}{2} \times 8 \times 4\sqrt{3} = 16\sqrt{3}=21​×8×43​=163​ cm² — the same as 34×82\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \times 8^243​​×82.

Common mistakes!

  • Teachers say that mixing up radius and diameter and mixing values of π, rounding early or writing the wrong unit are among the most common reasons for lost marks in this chapter.

  • "Diameter 42 cm", then π×422\pi \times 42^2π×422

    The formulas use the radius.

    Halve first: r=21r = 21r=21 cm, then π×212\pi \times 21^2π×212.

  • 227\dfrac{22}{7}722​ in one line and 3.14 in the next

    Two different values give two different answers.

    Use only the π the question gives, all the way through.

  • Rounding 227\dfrac{22}{7}722​ to 3.1 or rounding each line

    Small errors pile up.

    Keep fractions (or the given decimal) and round only the final answer.

  • Area written in cm, circumference in cm²

    An area is measured in square units.

    Lengths: cm, m. Areas: cm², m².

  • Turns of a wheel: km ÷ cm

    The two lengths must be in the same unit.

    Change km to cm (× 100 000) or cm to km first.

Key points

  • Circumference =2πr= 2\pi r=2πr; area =πr2= \pi r^2=πr2; diameter =2r= 2r=2r — halve a diameter before you start.
  • Use the π the question gives (227\dfrac{22}{7}722​, 3.14, or "in terms of π"), and round only at the end.
  • Ring: π(R2−r2)\pi(R^2 - r^2)π(R2−r2). Wheel: one turn = one circumference; turns =distancecircumference= \dfrac{\text{distance}}{\text{circumference}}=circumferencedistance​ in the same unit.
  • Triangle: 12×\dfrac{1}{2} \times21​× base ×\times× height; right triangle 12ab\dfrac{1}{2}ab21​ab; equilateral 34a2\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}a^243​​a2.
