Sample paper and case-study questions

12 questions from official CBSE sources. Each answer has two views: Understand it (every step) and Write it in the exam (what earns the marks).

Past board papers not yet available — to be added. This section currently holds the Areas Related to Circles items from the CBSE Mathematics Sample Question Papers 2026-27 and 2025-26 — Mathematics Standard (041) and Mathematics Basic (241) — answered with their official marking schemes (newest first). Past year board questions, and the "Exam focus" analysis, will be added once the papers are sourced.

Both 2026-27 sample papers state that there is no change in the question paper design and assessment pattern for 2026-27, so the 2025-26 items remain good practice. Standard and Basic share the same syllabus, so every student should try all twelve items. This chapter is part of Unit VI, Mensuration (10 marks, shared with Surface Areas and Volumes). It carries 4 marks in the 2026-27 Standard paper (two MCQs and a 2-mark question), 4 marks in the 2026-27 Basic paper (a case study), 4 marks in the 2025-26 Standard paper (two MCQs and a 2-mark question with an internal choice) and 5 marks in the 2025-26 Basic paper (an MCQ and a case study). Two items are alternatives for visually impaired candidates; each is given in full. Every paper says "Take π=227\pi = \dfrac{22}{7}π=722​ wherever required if not stated", so each item below uses 227\dfrac{22}{7}722​ unless its own text says otherwise.

Each item has two parts:

  • Understand it — the full step-by-step explanation: Given / Asked → Concept used (and why) → steps → Check → Common trap.
  • Write it in the exam — the answer as you should write it, following the official marking scheme, with marks shown. Where the scheme gives only a total for a group of lines, the split shown is inferred and marked so.
