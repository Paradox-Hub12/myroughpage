Words — a sector is the part of a circle between two radii and their arc; its angle θ is at the centre. A segment is the part between a chord and its arc. Each comes as minor (the smaller) and major (the larger); "sector" or "segment" alone means the minor one.

The whole circle (the "sector of 360°") — circumference 2πr2\pi r2πr, area πr2\pi r^2πr2; ring π(R2−r2)\pi(R^2 - r^2)π(R2−r2); one turn of a wheel = one circumference.
