Report a problem

Found a mistake?

Thank you — reports like yours make the site better for everyone. A wrong answer, a typo, a diagram that looks wrong or a button that does not work: all of these are worth telling us.

What to tell us

  1. The page — subject, chapter and section (for example: Maths · Chapter 14 · Practice).
  2. The question — its number on the page (for example P12, QC2, Exercise 14.1 Q8).
  3. What looks wrong — and what you think it should be, if you know.
  4. Your device — phone or computer, and the browser, if something does not display or work.

How to send it

A direct way to send a report will appear here when the site goes online. Until then, please pass it to your teacher.
