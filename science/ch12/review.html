Week 1

Set 2 — Week 1

5 questions, about 8 minutes

Focus: the reasons behind the facts.

W1.1 · 1 of 5●● Standard

Why can two magnetic field lines never cross?

Answer: At a crossing point a compass needle would have to point in two directions at once, which is not possible — the field at a point has only one direction. (Topic 1)

Did you get it?

W1.2 · 2 of 5●● Standard

A compass near a straight wire is deflected. Give two changes that would make the deflection smaller.

Answer: Reduce the current, or move the compass farther from the wire — the field is weaker for a smaller current and at a greater distance. (Topic 2)

Did you get it?

W1.3 · 3 of 5●● Standard

A beam of electrons moves up the page in a field pointing out of the page. Which way is it pushed?

Answer: The current is down the page (opposite to the electrons). Left hand: forefinger out of the page, middle finger down the page → thumb to the left. The beam is pushed to the left. (Topic 4)

Did you get it?

W1.4 · 4 of 5●● Standard

Why are metal-bodied appliances earthed?

Answer: If current leaks to the metal body, the earth wire carries it to the earth through a low-resistance path and keeps the body at earth potential, so the user does not get a severe shock. (Topic 6)

Did you get it?

W1.5 · 5 of 5●● Standard

State one difference between DC and AC, and one advantage of AC.

Answer: DC flows in one direction; AC reverses its direction periodically. Advantage of AC: power can be transmitted over long distances without much loss of energy. (Topic 5)

Month 1

Set 3 — Month 1

5 questions, about 12 minutes

Focus: direction rules and calculations — name the rule first.

M1.1 · 1 of 5●● Standard

A vertical wire carries a current downward. What is the direction of its field at a point just to the north of the wire?

Answer: Right thumb pointing down; seen from above the field is clockwise; just north of the wire it points east. (Topic 2)

Did you get it?

M1.2 · 2 of 5●● Standard

Seen from its right end, the current in a solenoid flows clockwise. Which end is the north pole?

Answer: Clockwise as seen → the field inside points away from you, to the left; the lines come out at the left end, so the left end is N. (Topic 3)

Did you get it?

M1.3 · 3 of 5●● Standard

A wire carrying current towards the west lies in a horizontal magnetic field pointing towards the south. Find the direction of the force on it.

Answer: Left hand: forefinger south, middle finger west → thumb vertically upwards. (Topic 4)

Did you get it?

M1.4 · 4 of 5●● Standard

A water pump is rated 1.155 kW, 220 V. What current does it draw, and can it run on the 5 A circuit?

Answer: I=1155220=5.25I = \dfrac{1155}{220} = 5.25I=2201155​=5.25 A; no — 5.25 A is more than 5 A, so it needs the 15 A circuit. (Topic 6)

Did you get it?

M1.5 · 5 of 5●● Standard

How many complete cycles, and how many changes of direction, does the 50 Hz supply make in 0.2 s?

Answer: Cycles =50×0.2=10= 50 \times 0.2 = 10=50×0.2=10; changes of direction =2×10=20= 2 \times 10 = 20=2×10=20. (Topic 5)
