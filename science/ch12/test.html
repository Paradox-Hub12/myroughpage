Section A · what to look at again

Question 1 · MCQ

The magnetic field lines of a bar magnet:

Correct: (b)

Field lines leave N, enter S and continue inside from S to N, so they are closed curves. (a) forgets the part inside the magnet; (c) reverses the outside direction; (d) describes only a uniform field.

Question 2 · MCQ

A long straight wire carries a current out of the page. Seen on the page, its magnetic field lines are:

Correct: (c)

Current towards you → right-hand thumb pointing at you → fingers curl anticlockwise. (a) is for current into the page; (b) and (d) are not the shape of the field round a wire.

Question 3 · MCQ

An electron moving towards the right across the page enters a magnetic field that points into the page. The force on the electron is:

Correct: (c)

The electron moves right, so the current is to the left. Left hand: forefinger into the page, middle finger to the left → thumb down the page. (b) comes from using the electron's direction as the current; (a) is along the line of motion; (d) is along the field.

Question 4 · MCQ

The earth wire connected to the metal body of an appliance:

Correct: (b)

The earth wire gives a low-resistance path to the earth and keeps the body at earth potential. (a) and (c) are the jobs of the live and neutral wires; (d) is exactly what earthing prevents.

Question 5 · A–R
AA compass needle placed near a wire is deflected when a current flows through the wire.
RThe magnetic field lines round a straight current-carrying wire are concentric circles.

Correct: (B)

A is true (Activity 12.1). R is true (Activity 12.5). But R only describes the shape of the field; the reason the needle is deflected is that the current produces a magnetic field that acts on the needle. So R does not explain A.

Question 6 · A–R
AThe 50 Hz AC supply in India changes its direction 50 times every second.
RAlternating current changes its direction twice in each cycle.

Correct: (D)

A is false: 50 Hz means 50 cycles a second, and with two changes per cycle the direction changes 100 times a second. R is true.
