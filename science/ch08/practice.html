P21●● StandardShort answer · 3 marksOriginal

"If each parent passed on all its genes as one single piece, an F1 plant RrYy (from RRyy × rrYY) could never make all four kinds of gametes." Explain this statement, and explain how chromosomes allow traits to be inherited independently.

Full solution

Given: a statement about gene sets. Asked: why it is true; how chromosomes solve it.

Concept used: chromosomes and independent inheritance (Topic 4). Why it applies: new combinations need genes to be sorted separately.

  1. 1

    One piece → genes that came in together from one parent (such as R and y from a round-green plant) would always go out together → they would be linked.

  2. 2

    Result → the F1 could make only the two gene sets it received, Ry and rY — never RY or ry. The F2 would be only RRyy, RrYy and rrYY (round green, round yellow, wrinkled yellow); wrinkled green (rryy) could never appear, and there would be no 9 : 3 : 3 : 1.

  3. 3

    Chromosomes → genes are on separate, independent pieces of DNA; each body cell has two copies of each chromosome, one from each parent.

  4. 4

    Germ cells → each takes one chromosome from each pair, maternal or paternal, separately for each pair → copies of different genes are sorted independently → new combinations.

  5. Check: this is why the F1 RrYy makes all four gametes RY, Ry, rY, ry ✔.

  6. Answer A single piece would keep genes linked, so the F1 could pass on only Ry and rY and wrinkled-green seeds could never appear. Because genes are on separate chromosomes, and each germ cell takes one chromosome of each pair independently, the traits are inherited independently and new combinations appear.

    Write it in the exam (3 marks):

    • Single piece → genes linked → F1 gametes only Ry, rY → no rryy, no 9 : 3 : 3 : 1. (1)
    • Genes are on separate chromosomes; two copies of each in body cells, one from each parent. (1)
    • Each germ cell takes one chromosome of each pair independently → independent inheritance. (1)
    Common trap: saying "each gamete gets half of each chromosome". It gets one whole chromosome from each pair.

E22●● StandardLong answer · 5 marksNCERT Exemplar Heredity and Evolution, LA 46

Give the basic features of the mechanism of inheritance.

Full solution

Given: heredity. Asked: the main features of how inheritance works.

Concept used: Topics 2–4. Why it applies: this collects the rules of the chapter.

  1. 1

    Genes control traits → each trait is controlled by a gene (a section of DNA giving one protein).

  2. 2

    Two copies → every individual has two copies of each gene, one from each parent; they may be the same or different.

  3. 3

    Dominant and recessive → if the two copies differ, the one that is expressed is dominant; the other is recessive.

  4. 4

    Genes on chromosomes → genes are carried on chromosomes, which come in pairs.

  5. 5

    One copy per gamete → the two copies separate when gametes form; each gamete gets one.

  6. 6

    Restored in the zygote → fertilisation brings two copies together again, one from each parent.

  7. Check: these rules give 3 : 1 and 9 : 3 : 3 : 1 ✔.

  8. Answer Traits are controlled by genes; each individual has two copies of each gene (one from each parent); if they differ, one (dominant) is expressed and the other (recessive) is not; genes lie on chromosomes; the two copies separate into different gametes; fertilisation brings them together again in the zygote.

    • Characters controlled by genes. (1)
    • Two copies of each gene, one from each parent. (1)

    • The two copies separate at gamete formation and come together in the zygote. (1)

Give reasons for the appearance of new combinations of characters in the F2 progeny.

Full solution
