Section A · what to look at again

Question 1 · MCQ

Pure tall and pure short pea plants are crossed, and their offspring are allowed to self-pollinate. In the next generation, what percentage of the plants carry one copy of T and one copy of t?

Correct: (b)

The first generation is all Tt; Tt × Tt gives TT, Tt, Tt, tt → Tt is 2 boxes of 4 → 24\dfrac{2}{4}42​ × 100% = 50%. 25% (a) is TT or tt; 75% (c) is all the tall plants; 100% (d) is the F1, not the F2.

Question 2 · MCQ

A tall pea plant with yellow seeds is known to be TtYY. Which combinations of the two genes will be found in its germ-cells?

Correct: (a)

The height copy is T or t; the colour copy is always Y → TY and tY. (b) needs a y copy, which the plant does not have; (c) and (d) put two copies of a gene in one gamete.

Question 3 · MCQ

An egg is fertilised by a sperm that carries a Y chromosome. The baby will be:

Correct: (c)

X from the egg + Y from the sperm = XY → a boy. (a) and (d) ignore the Y; (b) is wrong because the mother always gives X — the sperm decides.

Question 4 · MCQ

Pea plants with round, yellow seeds (RRYY) are crossed with plants with wrinkled, green seeds (rryy), and the F1 is self-pollinated. What fraction of the F2 seeds are expected to be round and green?

Correct: (d)

The F1 is RrYy; the F2 is 9 : 3 : 3 : 1 and round green is one of the "3" groups → 316\dfrac{3}{16}163​. 116\dfrac{1}{16}161​ (a) is wrinkled green; 916\dfrac{9}{16}169​ (c) is round yellow; 14\dfrac{1}{4}41​ (b) is a one-trait share.

Question 5 · A–R
ATwo black rabbits that have a white young one must each carry a hidden copy for white fur.
RA recessive trait is expressed only when both copies of the gene are recessive.

Correct: (A)

A is true: white appeared from black parents, so white is recessive and each parent hid one white copy. R is true and explains A: the young one is white only because it got a white copy from each parent.

Question 6 · A–R
AIn Mendel's experiment, the tall F1 plants had exactly the same genetic make-up as the pure tall parent.
RAll the F1 plants of a cross between pure tall and pure short plants were tall.

Correct: (D)

A is false: the F1 plants were Tt, while the pure tall parent was TT — when they self-pollinated, the F1 gave some short plants but the parent did not. R is true.
