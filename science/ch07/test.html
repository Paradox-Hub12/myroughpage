Section A · what to look at again

Question 1 · MCQ

A bee visits a mustard flower on one plant and then a mustard flower on a different plant, leaving some pollen on its stigma. What has the bee brought about?

Correct: (b)

Pollen carried from a flower of one plant to a flower of another is cross-pollination. Self-pollination (a) is within the same flower; fertilisation (c) is the later fusion of gametes in the ovule; germination (d) is the growth of a seed into a seedling.

Question 2 · MCQ

Some days after fertilisation, the petals and stamens of a flower have fallen off and one part at the base keeps swelling. That part is the:

Correct: (c)

After fertilisation the ovary grows and ripens into the fruit. The ovule (a) becomes the seed, not the fruit; the stigma (b) shrivels and may fall off; the anther (d) is part of the stamen, which falls off.

Question 3 · MCQ

A leaf that falls from a Bryophyllum plant onto moist soil soon has small plants growing from the notches along its edge. This is an example of:

Correct: (c)

New plants growing from buds on a leaf is vegetative propagation. Budding (a) is an outgrowth of an organism such as Hydra or yeast that detaches; regeneration (b) needs a cut body; fragmentation (d) is the breaking up of a simple body such as Spirogyra.

Question 4 · MCQ

Sperm formation needs a temperature lower than normal body temperature. Which structure provides this?

Correct: (b)

The scrotum holds the testes outside the abdominal cavity, where the temperature is lower. The prostate gland (a) adds fluid; the vas deferens (c) and urethra (d) are passages for sperms.

Question 5 · A–R
ARegeneration in Planaria is not the same as reproduction.
RMost organisms do not normally depend on being cut up in order to reproduce.

Correct: (A)

A is true: your textbook says regeneration is not the same as reproduction. R is true and is the reason your textbook gives.

Question 6 · A–R
AOral pills protect a woman from sexually transmitted infections.
ROral pills change the hormonal balance of the body so that eggs are not released.

Correct: (D)

A is false: pills are not a barrier, so they give no protection against STIs — only barrier methods help. R is true: that is how pills prevent pregnancy.
