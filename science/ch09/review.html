Day 1

Set 1 — Day 1

5 questions, about 5 minutes

Focus: the core facts you learnt yesterday.

D1.1 · 1 of 5● Foundation

Name the two easiest rays for drawing a concave-mirror ray diagram and say how each reflects.

Answer: A ray parallel to the principal axis reflects through F; a ray through C reflects back along itself. (Topic: Topic 2)

Did you get it?

D1.2 · 2 of 5● Foundation

In the New Cartesian Sign Convention, why is the object distance uuu always negative?

Answer: By convention the object always sits on the left of the pole (or optical centre), and distances to the left are negative. (Topic 4)

Did you get it?

D1.3 · 3 of 5● Foundation

Why does light bend when it passes obliquely from air into glass?

Answer: Its speed decreases in glass; the change in speed changes its direction — it bends towards the normal. (Topic 5)

Did you get it?

D1.4 · 4 of 5● Foundation

Which two devices always form a virtual, erect, diminished image?

Answer: A convex mirror and a concave lens. (Topic 3, Topic 6)

Did you get it?

A concave lens has a focal length of 50 cm. What is its power?

Answer: f=−0.50f = -0.50f=−0.50 m, so P=1−0.50 m=−2P = \dfrac{1}{-0.50\ \text{m}} = -2P=−0.50 m1​=−2 D. (Topic 7)

W1.4 · 4 of 5●● Standard

In a ray diagram for a convex lens, where should you show each ray bending, and which ray is drawn as a straight line through the lens?

Answer: Bend each ray once, at the lens's centre line (thin-lens rule). The ray through the optical centre O goes straight through without bending. (Topic 6)

W1.5 · 5 of 5●● Standard

Match each use with a mirror and give the reason: (a) rear-view mirror (b) torch reflector.

Answer: (a) Convex — always erect image, wider field of view. (b) Concave — bulb at F, so reflected rays come out parallel as a strong beam. (Topic 2, Topic 3)

Month 1

Set 3 — Month 1

5 questions, about 10 minutes

Focus: mixed, exam-style — the way board questions combine ideas.

M1.1 · 1 of 5●● Standard

A device has f=+25f = +25f=+25 cm. Is it a converging or diverging device if it is (a) a mirror, (b) a lens? Explain.

Answer: (a) Convex mirror — diverging (positive fff means F is behind the mirror). (b) Convex lens — converging (positive fff means the real focus is on the far side). The same sign means opposite behaviour for mirrors and lenses. (Wrap-up)

M1.3 · 3 of 5●● Standard

A ray enters a glass slab at 35°. State the angle of emergence and two features of the emergent ray.

Answer: 35°. The emergent ray is parallel to the incident ray and shifted sideways (lateral shift). (Topic 5)

M1.5 · 5 of 5●●● Advanced

A student says, "A convex lens always forms a real image and a concave mirror always magnifies." Evaluate both claims.

Answer: Both are wrong. A convex lens forms a virtual image when the object is between F1F_1F1​ and O. A concave mirror forms a diminished image when the object is beyond C. Image type and size depend on where the object is. (Topic 2, Topic 6)
