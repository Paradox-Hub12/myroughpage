Wrap-up

Mirror or lens?

About 30 minutes

In the exam, mirror and lens numericals sit side by side, and the commonest mistake is using the right numbers in the wrong formula. This page puts the two devices next to each other, gives you one routine for every numerical, and works through four mixed problems.

The comparison table

What happens to light reflects (bounces back) refracts (passes through)

Three things are the same for both: uuu is always negative; mmm negative always means real and inverted; mmm positive always means virtual and erect.

Two things swap: the sign in the formula (mirror +, lens −) and the sign in the magnification (mirror −, lens +).

Visual

The numerical routine — use it every time

  1. Mirror or lens? Which type? Write it down. This fixes the sign of fff and which formula you use.
  2. Sign table. uuu, vvv, fff (or RRR), hhh, h′h'h′ — with signs; "?" for unknowns. If RRR is given, f=R2f = \dfrac{R}{2}f=2R​ with the same sign. If power is given, f=1Pf = \dfrac{1}{P}f=P1​ in metres.
  3. Formula. Mirror: 1v+1u=1f\dfrac{1}{v} + \dfrac{1}{u} = \dfrac{1}{f}v1​+u1​=f1​. Lens: 1v−1u=1f\dfrac{1}{v} - \dfrac{1}{u} = \dfrac{1}{f}v1​−u1​=f1​. Rearrange for the unknown before substituting.
  4. Substitute with every negative value in brackets.
  5. LCM → simplify → flip.
  6. Magnification with the right formula (mirror −vu-\dfrac{v}{u}−uv​, lens vu\dfrac{v}{u}uv​); then h′=m×hh' = m \times hh′=m×h.
  7. Interpret: sign of vvv (where), sign of mmm (real/virtual, inverted/erect), size of mmm (enlarged/diminished).
  8. Check: substitute back into the formula; compare with the image table for that object position.

Mixed example 1

Same numbers, two devices

Question. An object is placed 20 cm from (a) a concave mirror of focal length 12 cm and (b) a convex lens of focal length 12 cm. For each, find the image distance and the magnification, and describe the image.

Given: object distance 20 cm; (a) concave mirror, f = 12 cm; (b) convex lens, f = 12 cm. Asked: vvv, mmm and the nature of each image.

Concept used: the numerical routine — mirror formula for (a), lens formula for (b). Why it applies: both are converging devices with the same focal length, so this shows exactly where the signs differ.

Mixed example 2

"f = +30 cm": which device?

Question. A spherical mirror and a thin lens each have a focal length of +30 cm. (a) Identify each. (b) An object is placed 60 cm in front of each. Find the image distance and magnification in each case and describe the images.

Given: mirror f=+30f = +30f=+30 cm; lens f=+30f = +30f=+30 cm; u=−60u = -60u=−60 cm for both. Asked: the type of each device; vvv, mmm and image descriptions.

Concept used: sign of fff identifies the type (mirror: + means convex; lens: + means convex); then the routine. Why it applies: "+30 cm" means different things for a mirror and for a lens.

Starting from power

Mixed example 4

Working backwards from an erect, enlarged image
