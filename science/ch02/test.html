Section A · what to look at again

Question 1 · MCQ

A solution turns phenolphthalein pink. Its pH could be:

Correct: (d) 11

Phenolphthalein is pink only in a base, so pH must be above 7. (a) and (b) are acidic; (c) is neutral — phenolphthalein stays colourless in all three.

Question 2 · MCQ

Plaster of Paris sets into a hard mass when mixed with water. How many molecules of water does each formula unit of CaSOX4\ce{CaSO4}CaSOX4​ gain as it sets?

Correct: (b)

Plaster of Paris (CaSOX4 ⋅ 12 HX2O\ce{CaSO4.1/2H2O}CaSOX4​⋅21​HX2​O) becomes gypsum (CaSOX4 ⋅ 2 HX2O\ce{CaSO4.2H2O}CaSOX4​⋅2HX2​O): it goes from ½ to 2 water molecules, a gain of 1½. (a) is the water PoP already holds; (b) is the "one whole water" slip; (d) is the total in gypsum.

Question 3 · MCQ

Marble chips are added to dilute hydrochloric acid. The gas given off:

Correct: (b)

Marble is calcium carbonate; with an acid it gives carbon dioxide, which turns lime water milky. (a) is hydrogen; (c) is oxygen; (d) treats the gas as basic; carbon dioxide is an acidic oxide.

Question 4 · MCQ

The aqueous solution of which salt turns red litmus blue?

Correct: (c)

KHCOX3\ce{KHCO3}KHCOX3​: weak acid (carbonic acid) + strong base (KOH\ce{KOH}KOH) → basic. (a) is neutral; (b) and (d) are acidic (strong acid + weak base).

Question 5 · A–R
AA solution of hydrochloric acid has a lower pH than a solution of acetic acid of the same concentration.
RHydrochloric acid is a strong acid and produces more HX+\ce{H+}HX+ ions than acetic acid at the same concentration.

Correct: (A)

Same concentration, but HCl releases far more HX+\ce{H+}HX+ → lower pH. R gives exactly that reason.

Question 6 · A–R
ADry hydrogen chloride gas turns dry blue litmus paper red.
RHydrochloric acid is a strong acid.

Correct: (D)

A is false: dry HCl has no HX+\ce{H+}HX+ ions, so dry litmus does not change. R is true: HCl is a strong acid (in water).
