(a) Biological magnification is the increase in the concentration of harmful non-degradable chemicals at each successive trophic level of a food chain. (b) The pesticide is washed into the soil and absorbed by the paddy plants along with water and minerals. The grasshoppers eat many plants, the sparrows eat many grasshoppers and the kites eat sparrows; the chemical is not broken down, so it accumulates at each level and is most concentrated in the kites (top, fourth level). (c) Going up the chain the energy decreases (about 10 % passed on at each step), while the pesticide concentration increases. (d) Use less (or safer) pesticide; wash and peel fruits and vegetables (helps, though it cannot remove everything).

Section A · what to look at again

Question 1 · MCQ

Which of the following is a correctly written food chain?

Correct: (d)

The chain starts with the producer (grass), and each arrow points to the eater: grass is eaten by zebras, zebras by the lion. (a) has every arrow reversed; (b) and (c) do not start with the producer and mix up the order.

Question 2 · MCQ

In a food chain, the primary consumers receive 730 kJ of energy. Using the 10 per cent law, roughly how much energy did the producers trap?

Correct: (b)

Down one level: × 10, so 730×10=7300730 \times 10 = 7300730×10=7300 kJ. (a) divides instead of multiplying; (c) takes 90 %; (d) multiplies twice (two steps instead of one).

Question 3 · MCQ

In the upper atmosphere, ozone is formed when:

Correct: (b)

UV splits OX2\ce{O2}OX2​ into free oxygen atoms, which combine with OX2\ce{O2}OX2​ to form OX3\ce{O3}OX3​. (a) CFCs are linked to ozone depletion; (c) and (d) are not how ozone forms.

Question 4 · MCQ

Which of the following groups contains only biodegradable items?

Correct: (c)

Dung, cotton and wood are all broken down by decomposers. (a) has a glass bottle; (b) has polythene; (d) has a plastic cup.

Question 5 · A–R
AFood chains generally consist of only three or four steps.
ROnly about 10 % of the energy at one trophic level is passed on to the next, so hardly any usable energy is left beyond the fourth level.

Correct: (A)

Both are true, and R is the reason your textbook gives for short food chains.

Question 6 · A–R
APesticide residues in food grains can always be removed by washing.
RPlants take up pesticides from the soil together with the water and minerals they absorb.

Correct: (D)

A is false — your textbook says residues cannot always be removed by washing. R is true.
