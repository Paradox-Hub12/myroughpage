Focus: applying the ideas — slightly harder.

W1.1 · 1 of 5●● Standard

Write the balanced equation: calcium hydroxide + hydrochloric acid → calcium chloride + water.

Answer: Ca(OH)X2+2 HCl→CaClX2+2 HX2O\ce{Ca(OH)2 + 2HCl -> CaCl2 + 2H2O}Ca(OH)X2​+2HCl​CaClX2​+2HX2​O (Topic 2, Topic 3)

Working:

  1. Formulae: calcium hydroxide Ca(OH)X2\ce{Ca(OH)2}Ca(OH)X2​; calcium chloride CaClX2\ce{CaCl2}CaClX2​ (Ca valency 2, Cl valency 1).
  2. Chlorine: 1 on the left, 2 on the right → put 2 in front of HCl\ce{HCl}HCl.
  3. Hydrogen on the left: 2 (in Ca(OH)X2\ce{Ca(OH)2}Ca(OH)X2​) + 2 (in 2 HCl\ce{2HCl}2HCl) = 4 → put 2 in front of HX2O\ce{H2O}HX2​O.
  4. Check: Ca 1 | 1; Cl 2 | 2; H 4 | 4; O 2 | 2 ✔.
Did you get it?

W1.2 · 2 of 5●● Standard

In the electrolysis of water, 30 mL of hydrogen is collected. How much oxygen is collected in the same time?

Answer: 15 mL — hydrogen : oxygen = 2 : 1, so oxygen = 302\dfrac{30}{2}230​ mL = 15 mL. (Topic 5)

W1.4 · 4 of 5●● Standard

In ZnO+C→Zn+CO\ce{ZnO + C -> Zn + CO}ZnO+C​Zn+CO, name the substance oxidised, the substance reduced and the reducing agent.

Answer: Oxidised: carbon (gains O). Reduced: zinc oxide (loses O). Reducing agent: carbon. (Topic 7)

Set 3 — Month 1

5 questions, about 10 minutes

Focus: mixed, exam-style — the way board questions combine ideas.

M1.1 · 1 of 5●● Standard

Balance: CX2HX6+OX2→COX2+HX2O\ce{C2H6 + O2 -> CO2 + H2O}CX2​HX6​+OX2​​COX2​+HX2​O (ethane burning).

Answer: 2 CX2HX6+7 OX2→4 COX2+6 HX2O\ce{2C2H6 + 7O2 -> 4CO2 + 6H2O}2CX2​HX6​+7OX2​​4COX2​+6HX2​O.

Working (Topic 3 — fuel rule: C, then H, then O):

  1. Carbon: 2 on the left → put 2 in front of COX2\ce{CO2}COX2​.
  2. Hydrogen: 6 on the left → put 3 in front of HX2O\ce{H2O}HX2​O.
  3. Oxygen on the right: 2 × 2 + 3 × 1 = 7. This is odd, but OX2\ce{O2}OX2​ can only supply an even number.
  4. So double everything found so far: 2 CX2HX6\ce{C2H6}CX2​HX6​, 4 COX2\ce{CO2}COX2​, 6 HX2O\ce{H2O}HX2​O.
  5. Oxygen on the right is now 4 × 2 + 6 × 1 = 14 → put 7 in front of OX2\ce{O2}OX2​.
  6. Check: C 4 | 4; H 12 | 12; O 14 | 14 ✔.
Did you get it?

M1.2 · 2 of 5●●● Advanced

Give the reaction 3 Fe+4 HX2O→FeX3OX4+4 HX2\ce{3Fe + 4H2O -> Fe3O4 + 4H2}3Fe+4HX2​O​FeX3​OX4​+4HX2​ two correct labels, with reasons.

Answer: Displacement — free iron takes hydrogen's place in water and hydrogen is set free. Redox — iron gains oxygen (oxidised); water loses oxygen (reduced). (Wrap-up)

M1.3 · 3 of 5●● Standard

Silver nitrate solution is added to sodium chloride solution. Describe what you see, write the equation, and give two types of reaction.

Answer: A white precipitate (silver chloride) forms. AgNOX3(aq)+NaCl(aq)→AgCl(s)+NaNOX3(aq)\ce{AgNO3(aq) + NaCl(aq) -> AgCl(s) + NaNO3(aq)}AgNOX3​(aq)+NaCl(aq)​AgCl(s)+NaNOX3​(aq) — double displacement and precipitation. (Topic 6)

Did you get it?

M1.4 · 4 of 5●● Standard

Explain why both corrosion and rancidity are called "effects of oxidation". Give one prevention for each.

Answer: In both, a substance reacts with oxygen in the air — the metal in corrosion, the fats and oils in rancidity. Prevention: corrosion — paint/oil/galvanise; rancidity — antioxidants/airtight storage/nitrogen flushing. (Topic 7)

Did you get it?

M1.5 · 5 of 5●●● Advanced

A student writes: "CaCOX3→CaO+COX2\ce{CaCO3 -> CaO + CO2}CaCOX3​​CaO+COX2​ and CaO+COX2→CaCOX3\ce{CaO + CO2 -> CaCO3}CaO+COX2​​CaCOX3​ are both decomposition reactions because they involve the same substances." Evaluate this statement.

Answer: Incorrect. The first is decomposition (one reactant → two products); the second is combination (two reactants → one product). Using the same substances does not make them the same type — the direction decides. They are opposite reactions. (Topic 4, Topic 5)
