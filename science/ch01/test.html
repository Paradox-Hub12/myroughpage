Section A · what to look at again

Question 1 · MCQ

Zinc granules are added to dilute hydrochloric acid. Which observation shows that the reaction is exothermic?

Correct: (c)

A rise in temperature means heat is released → exothermic. (a) bubbles show a gas is evolved, not the energy change. (b) zinc being used up shows a reaction, not that it is exothermic. (d) no colour change says nothing about heat.

Question 2 · MCQ

How many oxygen atoms are represented in 3 Ca(NOX3)X2\ce{3Ca(NO3)2}3Ca(NOX3​)X2​?

Correct: (c) 18

One Ca(NOX3)X2\ce{Ca(NO3)2}Ca(NOX3​)X2​: O = 3 × 2 = 6. Three units: 6 × 3 = 18. (a) forgets the coefficient; (b) multiplies only 3 × 3; (d) counts only the O in one nitrate.

Question 3 · MCQ

Which of these is a photolytic decomposition?

Correct: (b)

Light is the energy source. (a) and (d) use heat (thermal); (c) uses electricity (electrolytic).

Question 4 · MCQ

When sodium sulphate solution is mixed with barium chloride solution, the precipitate formed is:

Correct: (b)

NaX2SOX4(aq)+BaClX2(aq)→BaSOX4(s)+2 NaCl(aq)\ce{Na2SO4(aq) + BaCl2(aq) -> BaSO4(s) + 2NaCl(aq)}NaX2​SOX4​(aq)+BaClX2​(aq)​BaSOX4​(s)+2NaCl(aq) — barium sulphate is the insoluble white solid; sodium chloride stays dissolved. Yellow is the colour of lead iodide, not barium sulphate.

Question 5 · A–R
AWhen magnesium burns in air to form magnesium oxide, magnesium is oxidised.
ROxidation is the gain of oxygen by a substance.

Correct: (A)

Mg gains oxygen to become MgO\ce{MgO}MgO (A true). R is the definition that explains it.

Question 6 · A–R
APainting an iron gate prevents it from rusting.
RRusting is a decomposition reaction in which iron breaks down.

Correct: (C)

Paint keeps air and moisture away, so A is true. Rusting is iron combining with oxygen and water (an oxidation), not a decomposition, so R is false.
