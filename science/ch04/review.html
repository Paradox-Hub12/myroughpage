Write the balanced equation for the complete combustion of methane, and say what colour its flame is when the air supply is good.

Answer: CHX4+2 OX2→COX2+2 HX2O\ce{CH4 + 2O2 -> CO2 + 2H2O}CHX4​+2OX2​​COX2​+2HX2​O; a clean blue flame. (Topic 6)

W1.5 · 5 of 5●● Standard

Why do soap molecules form micelles in water?

Answer: The hydrocarbon tails are hydrophobic and do not dissolve in water, while the ionic heads are hydrophilic. The molecules cluster with tails inside (round any oily dirt) and heads outside in the water. (Topic 8)

M1.3 · 3 of 5●●● Advanced

A student says, "Ethane and ethene both react with hydrogen in the presence of nickel." Evaluate this.

Answer: Wrong. Only ethene (unsaturated) adds hydrogen. Ethane is saturated — there is no double bond to add across; it reacts with chlorine in sunlight by substitution instead. (Topic 6)

M1.5 · 5 of 5●●● Advanced

A shopkeeper says, "Use more soap in hard water and it will lather just like detergent." Evaluate this.

Answer: Partly true. Extra soap first reacts with the calcium and magnesium ions to form scum; only after those ions are used up does lather form — so soap is wasted and scum is left behind. A detergent forms no scum and lathers at once. (Topic 8)
