Section A · what to look at again

Question 1 · MCQ

In yeast, the first step of glucose breakdown takes place in the ______, and the final products are ______.

Correct: (b)

Glucose → pyruvate happens in the cytoplasm in every organism; in yeast, without oxygen, pyruvate → ethanol + carbon dioxide + energy. (a) and (d) put the first step in the mitochondria; (a) and (c) give the muscle product, lactic acid — and lactic acid is never made with carbon dioxide; (d) is aerobic respiration.

Question 2 · MCQ

Which enzyme is correctly matched with the organ that secretes it?

Correct: (c)

Lipase is in pancreatic juice. Pepsin comes from the gastric glands (a ✘); trypsin from the pancreas (b ✘); salivary amylase from the salivary glands (d ✘).

Question 3 · MCQ

Blood pumped out of the right ventricle goes:

Correct: (b)

The right ventricle pumps deoxygenated blood through the pulmonary artery to the lungs. (a) is the left ventricle's job; (c) the pulmonary vein brings blood from the lungs; (d) the vena cava brings blood to the heart.

Question 4 · MCQ

The movement of sucrose in the phloem from the leaves to the roots depends on:

Correct: (b)

Sucrose is loaded into phloem using ATP; the osmotic pressure this creates moves it. Transpiration pull and root pressure move water in xylem (a, c ✘); phloem moves food both up and down, so gravity is not the cause (d ✘).

Question 5 · A–R
AIn a nephron, blood is filtered in the tubular part.
RGlucose, amino acids, salts and much of the water are reabsorbed as the filtrate flows along the tubule.

Correct: (D)

A is false: filtration happens in the glomerulus/Bowman's capsule, not the tubule. R is true: the tubule carries out selective reabsorption.

Question 6 · A–R
AA destarched variegated leaf kept in sunlight for six hours turns blue-black with iodine only in its green parts.
RChlorophyll is necessary for photosynthesis.

Correct: (A)

Both are true, and R explains A: starch (blue-black) formed only where chlorophyll was present.
