Section A · what to look at again

Question 1 · MCQ

Which of the following is a lustrous non-metal?

Correct: (b)

Iodine is the lustrous non-metal. (a) Sulphur is a dull yellow non-metal. (c) Mercury is lustrous but it is a metal. (d) Oxygen is a colourless gas.

Question 2 · MCQ

A metal displaces lead from lead nitrate solution but does not displace zinc from zinc sulphate solution. The metal could be:

Correct: (b)

The metal is above lead but below zinc → iron (Zn > Fe > Pb). (a) and (d) are above zinc and would displace it. (c) Copper is below lead and cannot displace it.

Question 3 · MCQ

In the electron-dot diagram showing the formation of magnesium oxide, how many electrons are drawn around the oxide ion?

Correct: (c)

The oxide ion has a full octet: oxygen's 6 electrons plus the 2 it gained = 8. (a) is the number gained; (b) is the oxygen atom before reacting; (d) counts the inner shell too, which electron-dot diagrams never show.

Question 4 · MCQ

When an ore is heated strongly in limited air, it gives off a gas that turns lime water milky. The ore and the process are:

Correct: (b)

Lime water milky → carbon dioxide → a carbonate; heating a carbonate in limited air is calcination. (a) A sulphide gives sulphur dioxide. (c) Roasting is for sulphides. (d) Reduction turns an oxide into the metal and needs a reducing agent.

Question 5 · A–R
AGalvanised iron does not rust even when its zinc coating is scratched.
RZinc is more reactive than iron.

Correct: (A)

Both true. Because zinc is more reactive, it corrodes in place of the iron at the scratch — R explains A.

Question 6 · A–R
AIonic compounds conduct electricity in the solid state.
RIonic compounds are made of positive and negative ions.

Correct: (D)

A is false: in the solid the ions are fixed and cannot move. R is true: ionic compounds are made of ions (which conduct when molten or dissolved).
