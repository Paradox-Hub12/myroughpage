Section A · what to look at again

Question 1 · MCQ

In which of these situations are your ciliary muscles contracted the most?

Correct: (c)

The needle at about 25 cm is at the near point, where the eye lens must be thickest, so the ciliary muscles contract most. (a), (b) and (d) are distant objects, seen with the muscles relaxed.

Question 2 · MCQ

The far point of a myopic eye is 60 cm. The lens needed to correct it has a power of about:

Correct: (b)

Myopia: f=−60f = -60f=−60 cm =−0.6= -0.6=−0.6 m, so P=1−0.6≈−1.67P = \dfrac{1}{-0.6} \approx -1.67P=−0.61​≈−1.67 D. (a) has the wrong sign (that is a convex lens); (c) and (d) write the focal length in metres as if it were the power.

Question 3 · MCQ

In the spectrum of white light from a prism, which colour is bent more than green but less than indigo?

Correct: (b)

The order of bending, least to most, is red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet. Yellow and orange are bent less than green; violet more than indigo.

Question 4 · MCQ

Passengers flying at very high altitudes find that the sky looks dark. This is because:

Correct: (a)

There is very little air at such heights, so sunlight is hardly scattered and little light reaches the eye from the sky. (b) is wrong — sunlight is there; (c) blue is scattered, not absorbed; (d) is not the reason.

Question 5 · A–R
ATo see a rainbow in the spray of a fountain, you must stand with the Sun behind you.
RA rainbow is always formed in a direction opposite to that of the Sun.

Correct: (A)

A is true; R is true — it is your textbook's own statement — and it explains A: the rainbow forms opposite the Sun, so the Sun must be behind the observer.

Question 6 · A–R
AThe emergent ray from a triangular glass prism is parallel to the incident ray.
RAt the second face of the prism, light going from glass to air bends away from the normal.

Correct: (D)

A is false: the faces of a prism are inclined, so the emergent ray is deviated, not parallel. R is true.
