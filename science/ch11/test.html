Section A · what to look at again

Question 1 · MCQ

The SI unit of resistivity is:

Correct: (a)

From ρ=RAl\rho = \dfrac{RA}{l}ρ=lRA​, the unit is Ω×m2m=Ω\dfrac{\Omega \times \text{m}^2}{\text{m}} = \OmegamΩ×m2​=Ω m. (b) is the unit of resistance; (c) and (d) do not come from the formula.

Question 2 · MCQ

A charge of 90 C flows through a wire in 1.5 minutes. The current is:

Correct: (a)

t=1.5×60=90t = 1.5 \times 60 = 90t=1.5×60=90 s; I=Qt=9090=1I = \dfrac{Q}{t} = \dfrac{90}{90} = 1I=tQ​=9090​=1 A. (b) divides by 1.5 (minutes) instead of seconds; (c) multiplies; (d) divides the wrong way round.

Question 3 · MCQ

Resistors of 9 Ω and 18 Ω are connected in parallel. The equivalent resistance is:

Correct: (c)

1R=19+118=2+118=318\dfrac{1}{R} = \dfrac{1}{9} + \dfrac{1}{18} = \dfrac{2 + 1}{18} = \dfrac{3}{18}R1​=91​+181​=182+1​=183​, so R=6 ΩR = 6\ \OmegaR=6 Ω — less than 9 Ω. (a) is the series total; (b) the average; (d) is 1R\dfrac{1}{R}R1​, not flipped.

Question 4 · MCQ

An energy of 2.5 kW h is equal to:

Correct: (b)

2.5×3.6×106=9×1062.5 \times 3.6 \times 10^6 = 9 \times 10^62.5×3.6×106=9×106 J. (a) and (c) use wrong conversion factors; (d) is in watts — a unit of power, not energy.

Question 5 · A–R
AIn a string of bulbs joined in series, when one bulb fuses, all the other bulbs go off.
RIn a series circuit, when one component fails, the circuit is broken.

Correct: (A)

A is true: when one bulb of a series string fuses, the rest go dark (your textbook's fairy-lights example). R is true — it is your textbook's own statement about series circuits — and it explains A: the fused bulb breaks the only path, so no current flows through the others.

Question 6 · A–R
AWhen the potential difference across a resistor is doubled (its temperature remaining the same), its resistance also doubles.
RThe resistance of a conductor is given by R=VIR = \dfrac{V}{I}R=IV​.

Correct: (D)

A is false: at a steady temperature the resistance is constant; doubling the p.d. doubles the current. R is true: R=VIR = \dfrac{V}{I}R=IV​ — the ratio stays the same.
