About My Rough Page

What this is

My Rough Page is a free study site for CBSE Class 10 Science and Mathematics (Standard and Basic), written to the 2026-27 syllabus. There is nothing to buy and no sign-up.

What every chapter has

  • Learn — short topic lessons with diagrams, worked examples and quick checks.
  • NCERT — every textbook question, solved step by step so that a student who finds the subject hard can follow.
  • Practise — questions from easy to hard (about 30 % easy, 50 % standard, 20 % hard), with instant feedback.
  • Board questions — CBSE sample-paper and case-study questions, answered the way the official marking scheme gives marks.
  • Test — a timed chapter test in the board-exam pattern, and Revise — one-page summaries and quick recall.

How the content is made

Lessons and solutions are our own writing. Scope and facts are checked against the official NCERT textbooks and Exemplar, the CBSE 2026-27 syllabus and the CBSE sample papers and marking schemes. Every answer is worked out again independently before a chapter goes up, and teacher review of the chapters is ongoing.

Where an official book or paper has a mistake, we do not change it silently: you will see a short note next to the question.

Please note

My Rough Page is an independent, non-commercial site. It is not run by, or linked to, CBSE or NCERT. NCERT question text is shown so that you can study with the solution beside it; always follow your textbook, your teacher and the latest CBSE circulars.
